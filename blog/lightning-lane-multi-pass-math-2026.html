Lightning Lane Multi Pass math 2026: what a family of 4 actually pays.

This is the add-on, not a discount and not a touring plan. Multi Pass is a different price every park, every date. Here is the range, one quiet sample day, and when to leave it off the spreadsheet.

By Chris Bacon  ·  Founder, Vacation Math  ·  6 min read

Rooms and tickets get locked months out. Lightning Lane does not. Disney prices it by park and by date, shows those prices in the app about 21 days ahead, and still drops the charge on a trip you already thought was paid for. Genie+ is gone. Effective July 24, 2024, the paid products are Lightning Lane Multi Pass, Lightning Lane Single Pass, and Lightning Lane Premier Pass.

There is no hours-saved stat in this piece. Disney does not publish one, and a made-up “you save three hours” line would be fiction. The only question here is what the line item costs.

What you are actually buying

ProductWhat it coversHow it is priced
Multi PassUp to 3 Multi Pass experiences, with arrival windows, booked ahead in one park. After you redeem the first, you can pick more subject to availability (Park Hopper can pick in another park). Includes select PhotoPass perks.By theme park and date
Single PassOne in-demand attraction that is not on Multi Pass, with an arrival window. Up to 2 Single Passes per day, if they are available.By attraction and date
Premier PassOne-time entry to each available Lightning Lane experience in one park, one day, including Multi and Single attractions. No arrival windows. Limited quantity. Photo perks included.By theme park and date

Single Pass rides are not included in Multi Pass. As listed on Disney’s Lightning Lane page on September 22, 2026, the Single Pass–only attractions were Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and TRON Lightcycle / Run at Magic Kingdom, Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind at EPCOT, Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance at Hollywood Studios, and Avatar Flight of Passage at Animal Kingdom. Attraction lists change. Check the page before you assume a ride is in the bundle.

Date-based tickets outside a resort window open 3 days before the first ticket day, for the admission days on that ticket. Annual passes and other non-date-based tickets can buy any day inside a rolling 3-day window. You need valid park admission. A park reservation can also be required, depending on the ticket and the date.

The price is a range, not a rate card

Disney does not publish a fixed Multi Pass price list. The figures below are daily tracker ranges for the next roughly 31 days as of September 22, 2026 (Whistle While You Work, reading Disney booking data dated that day, alongside Thrill Data). They are before tax. They are not a quote for your dates.

The top of that Magic Kingdom band is not theoretical. Trackers listed MK at $39 on October 10, 11, and 16. Quieter late-September days were often $23–$29 there. Same product. Different day. Different bill.

Family of 4, one quiet sample

Tuesday, September 29, 2026 was a quiet-side day on those same trackers. Multi Pass before tax: Magic Kingdom $23, Hollywood Studios $22, EPCOT $18, Animal Kingdom $15. You cannot visit all four parks on that one Tuesday. The total below applies those four prices to one day in each park — a sample, not a reservation.

Multi Pass, family of 4, all four parks
September 29, 2026 tracker prices · one day per park · before you confirm in the app
Per person ($23 + $22 + $18 + $15)$78
Family of 4, before tax$312
After 6.5% ($312 × 1.065)~$332

That ~$332 is $332.28 rounded. Florida sales tax on this math is 6.5%: 6% state admissions tax plus Orange County’s 0.5% discretionary surtax for calendar year 2026. Walt Disney World also sits in Osceola County, where some purchases are taxed higher. For Lightning Lane and park passes, 6.5% is the rate this piece uses. The checkout line in My Disney Experience is the one that counts.

Multi Pass is not a trip discount. It sits on top of tickets you already bought.

Buy two parks, skip two

Same September 29 prices, Multi Pass on Magic Kingdom and Hollywood Studios only: $23 + $22 = $45 per person, $180 for four before tax, about $192 after 6.5% ($191.70). Against the four-park sample, that is $132 less before tax and about $141 less after tax ($140.58). EPCOT and Animal Kingdom are the days you are choosing to stand in line, or to rope-drop, instead of buying the pass.

One Single Pass on top

On that same September 29 sample, Rise of the Resistance was $22 per person before tax. Hollywood Studios Multi Pass was also $22. Stacked, that one park day is $44 per person before tax — $176 for four, about $187 after 6.5%. The other Single Pass samples that day, also before tax: TRON $19, Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind $18, Flight of Passage $15, Seven Dwarfs Mine Train $11. For the wider next-31-days window, trackers put Rise at about $20–$25, TRON $19–$23, Guardians $18–$22, Flight of Passage $15–$19, and Mine Train $11–$15.

Premier Pass is a different budget

Premier is the one-day luxury line, not the default family math. For the window priced through about October 13, 2026, Whistle While You Work listed Premier Pass before tax at $299–$429 at Magic Kingdom, $249–$349 at Hollywood Studios, $149–$249 at EPCOT, and $119–$199 at Animal Kingdom. Four people at the Magic Kingdom band is $1,196–$1,716 before tax for a single park day. That is the band times four, not a day Disney has quoted you. Inventory is limited. Confirm the day in My Disney Experience, then run it through the calculator before you call it worth it.

The calculator’s ~$27 is a blend

The Disney World Cost Calculator still shows Lightning Lane Multi Pass at about $27 per person per day. That mid is a blend. The four window averages above (~$32, ~$29, ~$25, ~$20) center near $26.50, which is why ~$27 still sits inside the band. It is not Disney’s price for every park and every date. Quiet Animal Kingdom on September 29 was $15. Peak Magic Kingdom days in this window were $39.

If you do use the blend as a placeholder — four people, four days, $27 — the line is $432 before tax and about $460 after 6.5%. Swap it for the prices the app shows on your dates. Do not budget the trip as if every day were $27.

Buy, skip, or stack

01
Buy Multi on Magic Kingdom and Hollywood Studios when the first selections are useful. Those two parks are the expensive end of the range. Buy the day if you can lock reservations you will actually redeem at purchase, and you expect to grab more after the first one is used. Short trips, and kids who will not survive a long standby, are the cases where the add-on earns its line. Still price that date. A $39 Magic Kingdom day is not a $23 day.
02
Skip Multi on quieter EPCOT and Animal Kingdom days. If rope-drop and standby are tolerable for your group, leave the pass off. On the September 29 sample those parks were $18 and $15 before tax. Skipping them is the $132 (before tax) in the example above.
03
Single Pass only for a must-do Multi does not include. Rise, TRON, Guardians, Flight of Passage, and Mine Train are the separate purchase. Buy one when the per-ride price still fits the trip, not because the screen offered it.
04
Treat Premier as a one-day luxury line. Four figures before tax for a family of 4 at Magic Kingdom is the ceiling, not the plan. Run that day through the calculator next to the room and the tickets.

What to do this week

01
Open My Disney Experience and price your park days. Disney shows Multi Pass prices about 21 days out. Write down the number for each date. Tracker ranges are a map. The app is the bill.
02
Decide one park day at a time. “Yes for the whole trip” is how a $15 Animal Kingdom day and a $39 Magic Kingdom day get the same answer. They should not.
03
Drop the days you are buying into the Disney calculator. Lightning Lane belongs next to tax, dining, and the room. Same spreadsheet as the rest of the trip. Use the ~$27 mid only until your dates replace it.
