"Up to 30% off" is marketing. Here's what the fall Disney room promo actually saves.

Most Moderate stays top out at 25% for 5+ nights, Florida still takes 12.5% tax, and the travel window ends October 3. The worked math for Caribbean Beach and Coronado.

By Chris Bacon  ·  Founder, Vacation Math  ·  5 min read

Disney's late-summer / early-fall room promo is still live, and the travel window closes soon: most arrivals have to be done by October 3, 2026. The marketing line says “up to 30% off.” For most Moderate stays, the real number is 25%. And then Florida takes a cut of whatever is left.

What the promo actually is

Per MouseSavers (updated July 30, 2026) and Disney Tourist Blog:

Stay Longer & Save More — Late Summer / Early Fall 2026
Most arrivals July 30 – October 3, 2026
Stay lengthMax discount (most resorts)Moderate (CBR / Coronado / POR)
5+ consecutive nightsUp to 30%Typically up to 25%
1–4 nightsUp to 25%Typically up to 20%
Arrivals Aug 14–27Lower percentages (peak of the window)

Costco Travel mirrors the same travel window and lists a book-by date of October 3, 2026. MouseSavers notes the offer can end at any time. If you're reading this after that date, the math still works for whatever promo replaced it — just swap in the new percent.

Tax is not optional

Disney's rack rates on MouseSavers are usually shown tax-in. Orange County lodging tax is still 12.5% (6% state + 0.5% surtax + 6% tourist development tax). All-Star Resorts sit in Osceola County at 13.5%. The discount applies to the room; tax is recalculated on the discounted subtotal. You do not get 25% off the tax line.

The worked example (Caribbean Beach, Fall)

MouseSavers lists Caribbean Beach Fall 2026 (Sep 6–Oct 1 and Oct 5–17) Standard Location weekdays at $364 per night tax-included. That's about $324 pre-tax before the 12.5%.

Coronado Springs Village Standard Fall weekdays publish at $332 tax-in (~$295 pre-tax). Same 5-night, 25% math lands around $1,245 tax-in and roughly $415 saved versus rack.

One more thing the marketing never says out loud: a 25% room discount is not a 25% trip discount. Tickets, dining, Lightning Lane, and flights don't move. On an $8,000–$10,000 all-in family trip, $450 off the room is real money — and it's still a single-digit percent of the whole vacation.

What to actually do

01
Arrive by October 3 if you want this promo. Columbus Day weekend (Oct 9–12) is outside the window. Fall rates without the discount are a different spreadsheet.
02
Take the 5th night if you're on the fence. For Moderates, 5+ nights unlocks the higher tier (typically 25% vs 20%). The extra night often costs less than the discount it unlocks on the whole stay.
03
Stack Kids Eat Free if you're on-site with kids 3–9. Disney's terms explicitly allow this promo to combine with Kids Eat FREE All Year in 2026. Almost nothing else stacks.
