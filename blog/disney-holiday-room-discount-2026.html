Disney’s 2026 holiday room discount: is 20% off worth booking?

The fall offer ended October 3. The holiday one runs through Christmas Eve. At the right resort it’s about $300 off a 4-night stay. At the wrong one it’s lunch money.

By Chris Bacon  ·  Founder, Vacation Math  ·  7 min read

Disney’s Stay Longer & Save offer ended October 3. If you’re pricing a Disney World trip between now and Christmas Eve, the public room discount you can book today is the holiday offer. It takes up to 20% off select rooms at Disney’s own hotels. The catch is in two words: “up to” and “select.” Some resorts get 20%. Some get 10%. A few room types get nothing.

I checked the terms and the rack rates on October 5, 2026. These are general-public numbers. Annual Passholders and Florida residents have their own bigger offers, and I cover those below. Disney limits how many rooms it sells at the discount, so price your own dates on Disney’s site before you count any savings.

What the offer actually covers

Disney lists two stay windows:

  • Most Sunday to Thursday nights, October 4 to November 19, 2026. Friday and Saturday nights in that stretch aren’t part of it.
  • Most nights, weekends included, November 22 to December 24, 2026. That covers Thanksgiving week and most of the Christmas season, up to Christmas Eve.
The ruleWhat it means for your family

1 to 14 nightsA two-night stay works. So does a long one. Unlike the fall offer, a longer stay doesn’t earn a bigger percent.
Doesn’t combine with other discountsThe one exception is Disney’s 2026 Kids Dining Plan offer: a free dining plan for kids 3 to 9 when everyone 10 and up buys a plan on a Disney package.
Limited number of roomsDisney sets aside a fixed number of discounted rooms. When they’re gone, the discount is gone for that night, even if the room is still for sale.
Lower savings on select dates“Up to 20%” can be less on some nights. The checkout screen tells you what you’re really getting.
More than 2 adults in a roomExtra per-adult charges can apply at Value, Moderate, and Deluxe rooms and Deluxe Villa studios.

Tax is figured on the discounted room. At Disney’s Orange County hotels that’s 12.5%: 6% Florida sales tax, 0.5% Orange County surtax, and 6% tourist development tax. So when the room drops 20%, the tax line drops 20% too.

Two things jump out. Riverside is the only Moderate where every room type is in the top tier. And the cheapest resorts got the smallest percent. If you booked a Value resort to save money, this offer helps you the least.

One real stay: Port Orleans Riverside, November 15 to 19

Here’s a stay I’d actually look at for a family of four: Port Orleans Riverside, Standard Location room with two queen beds. Check in Sunday, November 15. Check out Thursday, November 19. That’s four nights, Sunday through Wednesday, all inside the first window.

The holiday offer saves about $300 on this stay, if Disney gives the full 20% on these nights. That’s about $75 a night. Because tax is charged on the discounted room, you get 20% off the tax-included total. $1,502 times 0.8 is about $1,202.

“Up to” matters here. Disney says some dates get lower savings, and the discounted rooms are limited. If your checkout screen shows 15% instead of 20%, the savings on this stay drop to about $225. Price your exact dates on Disney’s site.

Same nights, other rates

If you hold an Annual Pass or live in Florida, the same four nights could cost less. November 15 to 19 sits inside a Florida resident window. The Value resort problem

Now the other end. A Pop Century Standard Room on an early November weeknight, Monday to Wednesday, is $241 a night with tax at rack, per MouseSavers. That’s about $214 before tax. Ten percent off saves about $21 before tax, or about $24 a night with tax.

Then there’s availability. Disney Tourist Blog searched the night of November 17 and couldn’t find Pop Century for under $214. That’s about Pop’s full weeknight rate before tax. It suggests the 10% was limited or already gone at Pop on that search. The cheapest rooms tend to go first.

I wouldn’t switch resorts or move dates to chase 10% at a Value. If Pop is already the plan and the discount shows up, take it. Just don’t build the trip around $24 a night.

Fall offer vs. holiday offer

The fall offer that ended October 3 paid more for longer stays. The holiday offer is flat. Here’s how a few resorts changed, using the published tiers. The fall numbers are for most nights July 30 to August 13 and August 28 to October 3. August 14 to 27 had lower percentages.

ResortStay Longer & Save (ended Oct. 3)Holiday offer (Oct. 4 to Dec. 24)

For a short Riverside stay, nothing changed. It was 20% then and it’s 20% now. Stay five nights or more and you lost 5 percentage points. Deluxe stays lost 5 to 10 points. Pop Century and All-Star Movies and Music went from 15% or 20% down to 10%. On a 5-night stay there, that’s half the old discount.

You can’t book the fall offer anymore, so this table is only a gut check. If a friend booked Pop at 20% off in September, that price is gone.

Book it or skip it

01
Book it when your dates fit and your resort is in the 20% tier. That means most Sunday to Thursday nights through November 19, or most nights from November 22. Deluxe, DVC, Riverside, or a Coronado Springs king room. On the 4-night Riverside sample, that’s about $300.
02
Price the same room three ways first. Room-only with the holiday offer. The same room as a package with tickets. And any Annual Passholder or Florida resident rate you qualify for. Take the lowest total, not the biggest percent.
03
Don’t chase 10% at a Value resort. About $24 a night at Pop Century is small money, and the discounted rooms may not be there. Book the resort that works for your family. Take the discount if it shows up.
04
Watch the weekend nights before November 22. Until then, Friday and Saturday nights are outside the offer. A Thursday-to-Sunday stay in early November only gets the discount on Thursday night.

What to do this week

01
Open Disney’s special offers page and price your exact dates. The percent on the checkout screen is the one that counts. “Up to” is the ceiling.
02
Already booked at full price? Ask. If your nights fall inside the windows, ask Disney or your travel agent whether the offer can be applied to the room you already have.
03
Put the room into the Disney calculator. Pick the resort tier and set the room discount to the percent you actually got: 10, 15, or 20. The room is one line. Tickets, food, Lightning Lane, and flights are the rest of the trip.
