Disney or Universal for 2027? The ticket math for four.

Disney’s 2027 prices are out. A three-day Universal trip and a five-day Disney trip for four are $872 apart before hotels, food, or parking.

By Chris Bacon  ·  Founder, Vacation Math  ·  8 min read

If you’re planning an Orlando trip for 2027, the question I hear most is whether Universal is really cheaper than Disney. Disney published its 2027 ticket prices on October 6, so I ran the numbers for a family of four. The honest answer is that a single day looks closer than most people expect, and the real difference shows up once you add days.

The prices below are starting prices on the cheapest dates, before tax, and both resorts price every date separately. Please treat them as floors and not as quotes for your dates.

What a single day costs

Disney’s one-day adult ticket starts at $119 before tax. With the 2027 calendar now out, the peak price is $229 at Magic Kingdom and EPCOT, up from $219 and $214 (WDWMagic, October 6). Universal’s one-day tickets start at $124.99 for Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure, and $139.99 for Epic Universe. Both resorts charge $35 to park a car.

On a single day, then, the cheapest Disney date and the cheapest Universal date are only about $6 apart, and Disney’s peak dates cost far more than either. If you’re only going for one day, the date you pick matters as much as the resort.

Where the bills really split

The difference shows up when you add days. As of October 29, Whistle While You Work lists Universal’s three-day ticket from $297 per adult, with one day in each of the three parks, and Disney’s cheapest three-day ticket at $380.88. The Universal ticket is a starting price with blockout dates, so check that your dates are eligible.

The other thing that changes the math is how many days you actually need. Universal’s parks fit comfortably into two or three days, while Disney’s four parks usually take four or five. So the fairer comparison is often a short Universal trip against a longer Disney one.

Those four-ticket totals are my multiplication of the starting prices, with all four tickets priced as adults. The Disney five-day figure comes from orlandobestplace, checked October 6, and it works out to about $103 a day per ticket. An $872 gap is real money, but it’s before hotels, food, or parking, and your own dates will move both sides.

What the ticket price leaves out

Tickets are only part of the bill. Parking is $35 a day at either resort if you drive. If skipping lines is part of your plan, Disney’s Lightning Lane Multi Pass runs $15 to $42 per person, per day, which I broke down in my Lightning Lane post. Universal sells its own line-skipping add-on too, so price that on both sides before you decide which resort is cheaper. Neither is included in the ticket totals above.

How I’d pick

For a long weekend with teens, Universal’s math is hard to beat, because three parks in three days fits the budget and the energy. For little kids, or for any trip of five days or more, I’d still lean toward Disney, since the pace is gentler and there’s more to fill the time. That’s my opinion and not a calculation, so weigh it against what your family actually likes.

Quick answers

Is Universal Orlando cheaper than Disney World in 2027?

Per day the cheapest tickets are close: Disney starts at $119 and Universal at $124.99. The gap opens with multi-day tickets. A three-day Universal ticket starts at $297 per adult, against $380.88 for Disney’s cheapest three-day ticket.

What is Disney’s highest one-day ticket price for 2027?

$229 at Magic Kingdom and EPCOT on peak dates, up from $219 and $214, according to WDWMagic’s October 6, 2026 report.

How much would a family of four pay for tickets?

Using starting prices for four adults, three days at Universal comes to $1,188 and five days at Disney comes to about $2,060, before tax, hotels, food, or parking.
