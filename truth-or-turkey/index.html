Truth or Turkey

A bluff you play from your phone while you eat. Two lines each turn: one true, one made up. The table has to tell them apart.

  1. 1Write one truth and one turkey, the fake.
  2. 2Read both out loud for 30 seconds.
  3. 3Everyone else taps the true one.
  4. 4A point for each person you fool. A point for each truth you spot.

Rounds, in order: Thankful, Thanksgivings past, Food confessions, Kid me. Awards: Top Truth Teller and Biggest Turkey.
