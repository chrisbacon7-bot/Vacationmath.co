What can we afford — common questions

What can we afford for a vacation in 2026?

Start with the cash, not the destination. The calculator ranks Disney, cruises, all-inclusives, city trips, road trips, and theme parks that fit the number you type, using the same 2026 prices as the other tools. A $3,000 trip and a $10,000 trip are different products.

How is this different from Trip Finder and Trip Plan?

Reverse budget starts with the money and ranks trip types. Trip Finder starts with a date window and ranks destinations. Trip Plan starts with a place you already want. Use this page when the question is what the cash buys.

What does a $6,000 vacation budget buy?

The form defaults to $6,000 because that is where the ranking usually changes. A couple can often hold a mid-range cruise or all-inclusive. A family of four is usually choosing a value Disney week, a shorter trip, or a drive. Run your party size. The result is the answer.

Should the vacation budget include flights?

Yes. The number should be everything you will spend: getting there, lodging, food, tickets, and the fees people add later. Pick the departure airport so airfare is inside the trip totals.
