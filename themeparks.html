How we got these numbers

18 parks with 2026 pricing data (updated May 2026). Ticket data sourced from each park's published 2026 rates and current promotions (Disney 4-Park Magic, Universal multi-day, SeaWorld Memorial Day sales, Six Flags advance pricing, Cedar Point season passes). Hotel rates use on-property published averages where available, and nearby offsite averages otherwise. Child tickets default to 90% of adult (Disney/Universal pattern); free/discounted children where the park offers it are noted in the result. Results are estimates, not live quotes.
