Tokyo

A station business hotel and konbini breakfast can make Tokyo cheap. The overrun is a hotel buffet plus a JR Pass on a city-only week.

Who this is for

For

Not for

  • Anyone faking Kyoto as a Tokyo day
  • People buying a JR Pass for a 5-night city trip

Getting around

Suica / PASMO on JR and Metro. Station downstairs is the lodging product. Taxis are Splurge.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • IC card (Suica / PASMO / Welcome Suica). Tap and stop thinking
  • Shinjuku, Shibuya, Ueno, or Tokyo Station — pick a ward and walk it
  • NRT vs HND: Haneda is the closer gift. Narita is a Skyliner or N’EX line you should price

Where to stay

APA, Super Hotel, or Toyoko Inn next to Shinjuku or Ueno. Capsule only if you packed a cube.

Budget

  • APA, Super Hotel, or Toyoko Inn in Shinjuku or Ueno station downstairs, konbini in the lobby
  • Sakura Hotel Jimbocho or a hostel-plus in Asakusa walk to a Metro

Mid-range

  • Mitsui Garden Shibuya or Ginza Premier Metro in the basement
  • Hotel Gracery Shinjuku or The Knot Tokyo neighborhood walking, not a taxi

Splurge

  • Park Hyatt Shinjuku the view; you still take the Metro to dinner
  • Aman Tokyo or Palace Hotel Otemachi / Imperial-garden quiet

3-day skeleton

Day 1

The ward you booked

  • Shinjuku or Shibuya on foot
  • Convenience-store picnic in a park
  • Night walk, one izakaya, Metro home — there is no Kyoto today

Day 2

Asakusa morning, one ticketed indoor

  • Senso-ji and the river early
  • Then teamLab or a museum — one, off-peak if you can
  • Evening back in your ward

Day 3

A quiet ward — or Kamakura if you already priced it

  • Yanaka or Shimokitazawa / Kichijoji for a free morning
  • Kamakura only if you accept a half-day out of the city
  • Do not fake Kyoto

Book before you go

  • One sushi counter if that is the treat — before you land
  • Welcome Suica or IC card
  • teamLab timed entry if that is the indoor
  • Haneda if you can; price Narita Skyliner / N’EX if you cannot

Hidden costs

  • Hotel breakfast buffet vs konbini
  • JR Pass on a city-only week
  • Taxis invented because the hotel is not on a station
  • Narita ground vs Haneda
  • Kyoto day that is actually a station and a blur

Skip this

  • The hotel breakfast buffet — Konbini is better and cheaper. This is not a compromise.
  • A JR Pass on a 5-night city trip — IC card + Metro is the plan. The pass is a souvenir spreadsheet.
  • Kyoto as a Tokyo day — That is a different trip. You will see a station and a blur.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Convenience-store onigiri and coffee for breakfast. Hotel buffets are the expensive path.
  2. A JR Pass is usually a bad buy on a 5-night city trip. IC card + Metro is the plan.
  3. APA / Super Hotel / Toyoko Inn next to a station on Budget. Capsule only if you packed a cube.
  4. Conveyor sushi and ramen are Mid-range lunches. Book one sushi counter if you already priced it — before you land.
  5. Skip Golden Week and peak blossom weekends unless that is the trip. Late January is the value window.
  6. Yanaka or Shimokitazawa mornings are free. teamLab or a tower is one ticketed indoor.
  7. Kyoto is a different trip. Do not fake it as a Tokyo day.
  8. The room or the counter, rarely both. Park Hyatt views still end with a Metro ride to dinner.
