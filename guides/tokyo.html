Tokyo vacation costs: stay, eat, get around (2026)

A station hotel and a konbini breakfast can make Tokyo the cheap version of itself. A hotel buffet and a JR Pass on a city-only week are the expensive version.

Start here APA, Toyoko Inn, or Mitsui Garden over a JR or Metro line. Onigiri from the lobby konbini. One ward today.

Getting around

Suica / PASMO on JR and Metro. Station downstairs is the lodging product. Taxis are Splurge.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • IC card (Suica / PASMO / Welcome Suica). Tap and stop thinking
  • Shinjuku, Shibuya, Ueno, or Tokyo Station — pick a ward and walk it
  • NRT vs HND: Haneda is the closer gift. Narita is a Skyliner or N’EX line you should price

Where to eat

Onigiri and coffee. Conveyor sushi or ramen at lunch. Golden Gai or Omoide Yokocho for the izakaya. Sushi Saito or a Ginza counter only if it was booked before the flight.

Budget

  • Onigiri and coffee from the station konbini
  • Conveyor sushi or a standing sushi counter
  • A ramen shop or a gyudon chain in the ward you booked

Mid-range

  • A depachika lunch in the station
  • Golden Gai or Omoide Yokocho one izakaya
  • A kissaten breakfast the morning you skip the konbini

Splurge

  • Sushi Saito or a Ginza counter only if it was booked before the flight
  • A Toyosu outer-market counter if that booking exists
  • The hotel breakfast buffet is a second room charge

Things to do

Walk the ward you booked. Sensō-ji at opening is free. teamLab Planets is the timed ticket. Kamakura is a half-day, not a fake Kyoto.

3-day skeleton

Day 1

The ward you booked

  • Konbini breakfast
  • Shinjuku or Shibuya on foot
  • Golden Gai or Omoide Yokocho — one izakaya

Day 2

Asakusa, or teamLab

  • Sensō-ji at opening, free
  • teamLab Planets only if that timed ticket is the indoor
  • Not both plus a Kamakura afternoon

Day 3

A quieter ward

  • Yanaka or Shimokitazawa in the morning
  • Sushi Saito or a Toyosu outer-market counter only if the booking exists
  • No JR Pass math on a city day

Book before you go

  • One sushi counter if that is the treat — before you land
  • Welcome Suica or IC card
  • teamLab Planets timed entry — if that is the indoor ticket
  • Haneda if you can; price Narita Skyliner / N’EX if you cannot

Hidden costs

  • Narita Skyliner or N’EX fare if you did not fly Haneda
  • A hotel breakfast buffet versus konbini prices
  • A JR Pass on a Tokyo-only week
  • teamLab timed entry on top of a museum the same day
  • A small accommodation tax outside the rate that already includes consumption tax

Skip this

  • A JR Pass for a city week — Suica or PASMO already covers the rides you will take.
  • The hotel buffet — The konbini is in the station you slept above.
  • Kyoto as a Tokyo day — Kamakura is the half-day. Kyoto is a different trip.

Suica, not a JR Pass

Top money-saving tips

  1. Hidden gem: Buy Suica or PASMO for Tokyo. A JR Pass on a city-only week is a national ticket you will not ride.
  2. Hidden gem: Haneda is the closer airport. Add the Narita Skyliner or N'EX fare before a cheaper Narita flight wins.
  3. Hidden gem: Sensō-ji at opening does not charge admission. teamLab Planets is the timed ticket. Do not buy both and a Kamakura afternoon.
  4. Book the sushi counter before you land, or eat conveyor sushi and keep the room.
  5. Yanaka or Shimokitazawa is the free afternoon. Ginza is only the counter you already booked.
