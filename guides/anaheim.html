This lodging is Anaheim

Disneyland is a walkable Harbor Blvd problem, not a Los Angeles itinerary. You sleep within a shuttle or a stroll of the gates, grocery breakfast, and treat Downtown Disney as a dinner — not a meal plan. Base yourself here

If you cannot walk or shuttle to the gate, you booked the wrong city.

  • Harbor Blvd limited-service — walk or ART, grocery in the room.
  • Grand Californian or Pixar Place if leftover is real. Still Anaheim, still no LA day stacked in.

Three days, two parks, no invented third

Sleep in Anaheim. Walk or shuttle. Stop stacking Los Angeles.

Day 1

Disneyland park, then the hotel

Rope drop one land you actually care about. Grocery breakfast, mobile-order lunch, Downtown Disney only if leftover covers dinner. You are not driving to Santa Monica tonight.

Day 2

California Adventure, or the other park

One park. Lightning Lane only if this is the crowded day and you already priced it. Evening on Harbor Blvd or the hotel courtyard — you already paid for the walk.

Day 3

A second single-park day — or go home

Do not Hopper this unless you will switch after lunch on purpose. Universal is not this day. Downtown Disney is not a third park.

Leave it off the card

  • Universal as a “quick” add-on — That is a Los Angeles ticket with its own math. Do not cram it into three Disneyland nights.

Walk, shuttle, done

Walk or take the hotel shuttle. This lodging is Anaheim — not a Getty / Griffith / Universal stack from a Santa Monica hotel.

  • Harbor Blvd and Downtown Disney are walkable. A rental car is a parking line if you never leave the bubble.
  • ART (Anaheim Resort Transportation) if your hotel is a few blocks off the walk.
  • SNA is the close airport. LAX is cheaper air and a longer ground transfer — price both.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. One park per day on Budget. Hopper only if you will switch after lunch — the campus is walkable, the ticket is not free.
  2. Lightning Lane on the Disneyland park day, not both days, unless leftover is real.
  3. Grocery breakfast (Albertsons / Target) plus mobile-order QS. Downtown Disney is a dinner, not a meal plan.
  4. Stay in Anaheim. A Santa Monica hotel plus a 90-minute transfer is not a Disneyland trip.
  5. Midweek SNA or LAX flights. Friday arrivals into Halloween Time are a tax.
  6. Universal Studios is a Los Angeles day with its own ticket math — do not stack it into a 3-night Disneyland stay.
  7. The dining plan is still usually a bad buy. Pay as you go.
  8. Pixar Place or Grand Californian is leftover. Budget is Harbor Blvd and a grocery bag.
