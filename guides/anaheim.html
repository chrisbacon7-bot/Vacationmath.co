Who this is for

For

  • People who will sleep within a walk or ART shuttle of the gates
  • Anyone who wants two parks without inventing a Los Angeles itinerary

Not for

  • Anyone booking Santa Monica and calling it a Disneyland trip
  • People stacking Universal into three Disneyland nights

Getting around

Walk or take ART. Skip a rental if you stay in Anaheim; a car is a parking line unless you leave the bubble on purpose.

No rental

  • Harbor Blvd and Downtown Disney are walkable
  • ART (Anaheim Resort Transportation) if your hotel is a few blocks off the walk
  • SNA is the close airport. LAX is cheaper air and a longer ground transfer — price both

With a rental

  • Hotel parking is a line if you never leave the bubble
  • Still one base in Anaheim. Use the car for a planned grocery run, not a nightly Santa Monica dinner
  • Universal is a Los Angeles ticket with its own parking

Where to stay

A Harbor Blvd 3-star and a grocery bag. Grand Californian is Splurge. Santa Monica is a different city.

Budget

  • Candy Cane Inn Harbor Blvd courtyard; walk or a short shuttle to the gates
  • Tropicana Inn or Castle Inn value room, request away from the boulevard if you can

Mid-range

  • Pixar Place Hotel on-property walk to Downtown Disney
  • Hotel Lulu or Hilton Anaheim walk or shuttle; still not a Los Angeles itinerary

Splurge

  • Grand Californian walk to California Adventure
  • Disneyland Hotel on-property campus; do not add a Santa Monica night

Where to eat

Albertsons or Target the night you land. Downtown Disney is a dinner, not a dining plan.

Budget

  • Albertsons or Target the night you land breakfast in the room
  • Mobile-order QS in the park you already paid to enter

Mid-range

  • Downtown Disney sit-down one dinner, not a dining plan
  • Carthay Circle or Lamplight Lounge one table-service if you already priced it

Splurge

  • Napa Rose one reservation, booked before you fly
  • Character breakfast only if that morning is the trip still pay as you go

Things to do

One park per day on Budget. Hopper only if you will switch after lunch.

Budget

  • Disneyland park as day one rope drop one land, no Hopper
  • Downtown Disney evening instead of a third ticket

Mid-range

  • California Adventure as the second park day
  • Lightning Lane on the Disneyland park day only

Splurge

  • Hopper only if you will switch after lunch the campus is walkable, the ticket is not free
  • World of Color dining only if you already priced it Universal is a Los Angeles ticket

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Disneyland park, then the hotel

  • Rope drop one land you actually care about
  • Grocery breakfast, mobile-order lunch
  • Downtown Disney only if dinner is already priced — you are not driving to Santa Monica

Day 2

California Adventure, or the other park

  • One park
  • Lightning Lane only if this is the crowded day
  • Evening on Harbor Blvd or the hotel courtyard

Day 3

A second single-park day — or go home

  • Do not Hopper unless you will switch after lunch on purpose
  • Universal is not this day
  • Downtown Disney is not a third park

Book before you go

  • Park tickets before you fly
  • Lightning Lane on the Disneyland park day only, unless you already priced both
  • One table-service if you want Carthay Circle or Napa Rose
  • ART pass if your hotel is off the walk

Hidden costs

  • Hotel parking if you rent a car you will not use
  • Sales tax on tickets
  • Lightning Lane and Hopper if you tap yes
  • LAX ground transfer vs SNA air
  • Bags if you flew cheap and now need an Uber van

Skip this

  • The dining plan — Pay as you go. It is still usually a bad buy.
  • Universal as a “quick” add-on — That is a Los Angeles ticket. Do not cram it into three Disneyland nights.
  • A Westside hotel — You will spend the value of a park ticket sitting in traffic.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. One park per day on Budget. Hopper only if you will switch after lunch.
  2. Lightning Lane on the Disneyland park day, not both days, unless you already priced it.
  3. Grocery breakfast (Albertsons / Target) plus mobile-order QS. Downtown Disney is a dinner, not a meal plan.
  4. Stay in Anaheim. A Santa Monica hotel plus a 90-minute transfer is not a Disneyland trip.
  5. Midweek SNA or LAX flights. Friday arrivals into Halloween Time cost more.
  6. Universal Studios is a Los Angeles day with its own ticket math.
  7. The dining plan is still usually a bad buy. Pay as you go.
  8. Grand Californian or Pixar Place is Splurge. Budget is Harbor Blvd and a grocery bag.
