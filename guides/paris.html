Who this is for

For

  • People who will pick an arrondissement they can walk in slippers and tap the Metro without thinking
  • Anyone who wants one timed museum and dinner on the block

Not for

  • Anyone who wants a rental car in Paris
  • People stacking four museums and a tower-steps formule in one day

Getting around

Navigo or a carnet. Stairs are the elevator. The tower is a Trocadéro view on Budget, not a summit default.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • Metro + RER. A Navigo week (if your days qualify) beats a fistful of t+ tickets
  • Walk one arrondissement in the morning. Pack light — stairs are common
  • CDG vs ORY: RER B is the honest CDG move. A taxi is Splurge, not Budget

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Your arrondissement, then the river

  • Bakery crawl on the block
  • Île de la Cité / Left Bank walk
  • Eiffel from Trocadéro at dusk — do not eat on the steps

Day 2

One timed museum

  • Louvre or Orsay — you already picked
  • Late afternoon in the Marais or along the Canal
  • Neighborhood dinner

Day 3

A cemetery, a smaller room, or Versailles if you already priced it

  • Père Lachaise or a second smaller museum (not a third mega)
  • Versailles only if you accept the RER half-day
  • Evening: fromagerie, wine, stairs, bed

Book before you go

  • Louvre or Orsay timed ticket — pick one
  • One 10th–11th dinner if you want Septime / Frenchie / Le Comptoir
  • Navigo if your days qualify, otherwise a carnet
  • Atlantic crossing 2–4 months out, midweek. Open-jaw (in Paris, out Rome) often beats two one-ways

Hidden costs

  • VAT in the room rate — still not a US-style surprise, but mid already eats a US city budget
  • Taxis from CDG vs RER B
  • Tower summit + Sainte-Chapelle stacked as “both”
  • Museum-steps formules
  • Bags on stairs if you packed like a palace

Skip this

  • Restaurants on the tower, the hill, or the museum steps — A tourist menu with a view surcharge. Walk five minutes.
  • A four-museum day — One timed ticket. The rest is walking you will actually remember.
  • A Gare hotel for the whole week — Fine for an early arrival. Sad as a neighborhood.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Bakery breakfast on your block. Du Pain et des Idées class if you slept in the 10th — not the hotel buffet.
  2. Bouillon Chartier / Pigalle or a formule du midi. Skip restaurants on the tower, the hill, and the museum steps.
  3. Navigo or a carnet. Taxis are Splurge. Pack light — stairs are common.
  4. Louvre or Orsay — pick one timed ticket. A four-museum day is how you buy souvenirs you will not remember.
  5. Eiffel from Trocadéro or Champ de Mars on Budget. The summit is a pick-one with Sainte-Chapelle.
  6. Book the Atlantic crossing 2–4 months out, midweek. Open-jaw (in Paris, out Rome) often beats two one-ways.
  7. Fromagerie + wine is a valid dinner. Palace-hotel dining only if you already priced it.
  8. 10th–11th or 18th–19th on a Metro line. A tower-block view is a tourist markup.
