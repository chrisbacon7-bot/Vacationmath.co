Who this is for

For

  • People who will pick an arrondissement they can walk in slippers and tap the Metro without thinking
  • Anyone who wants one timed museum and dinner on the block

Not for

  • Anyone who wants a rental car in Paris
  • People stacking four museums and a tower-steps formule in one day

Getting around

Navigo or a carnet. Stairs are the elevator. The tower is a Trocadéro view on Budget, not a summit default.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • Metro + RER. A Navigo week (if your days qualify) beats a fistful of t+ tickets
  • Walk one arrondissement in the morning. Pack light — stairs are common
  • CDG vs ORY: RER B is the honest CDG move. A taxi is Splurge, not Budget

Where to stay

Ibis or a walk-up near République or Oberkampf. A canal boutique in the 10th or 11th if you want dinner on the block. Crillon or Bristol when the palace is the point, not the default.

Budget

  • Ibis or Hotel F1 on a Metro line in the 10th, 18th, or 19th bakery downstairs
  • Generator Paris or a 2-star walk-up near République / Oberkampf

Mid-range

  • Hôtel Malte or an Odéon-class 3-star walk to a Metro and a market street
  • Canal Saint-Martin boutique in the 10th–11th dinner on the block

Splurge

  • Lutetia-class Left Bank only if the name is the point
  • Hôtel de Crillon or Le Bristol 1st / 8th address; palace breakfast is a line

Where to eat

Bakery and coffee every morning. Bouillon Chartier or a formule for lunch. A fromagerie on Rue du Faubourg-Saint-Denis is a real dinner. Le Clarence or Epicure is palace dining, its own line.

Budget

  • Bakery plus coffee on your block Du Pain et des Idées class if you slept in the 10th
  • Bouillon Chartier / Pigalle or a formule du midi not the tower steps

Mid-range

  • A fromagerie on Rue du Faubourg-Saint-Denis plus a bottle a valid dinner
  • One 10th–11th reservation Septime / Frenchie / Le Comptoir if you booked it

Splurge

  • Le Clarence at Hôtel de Crillon or Epicure at Le Bristol palace dining is its own line
  • Skip an Eiffel restaurant, a Montmartre terrace menu, and a Louvre-steps formule even on Splurge

Things to do

Trocadéro at dusk is the tower view. Pay for the Louvre or the Orsay, not both the same day. Versailles is an RER half-day.

Budget

  • Eiffel from Trocadéro or Champ de Mars dusk, no restaurant on the steps
  • Île de la Cité / Left Bank walk from your arrondissement

Mid-range

  • Louvre or Orsay pick one timed ticket
  • Père Lachaise or a smaller room not a third mega-museum

Splurge

  • Versailles only if you accept the RER half-day
  • Tower summit or Sainte-Chapelle pick one, not both stacked

3-day skeleton

Day 1

The block, then one museum

  • Bakery and coffee downstairs in the 10th or 11th — not a hotel breakfast
  • Louvre or Musée d’Orsay — one timed ticket
  • Dinner on the same Metro line, not a restaurant on the museum steps

Day 2

Trocadéro, no summit

  • Eiffel Tower from Trocadéro or Champ de Mars at dusk
  • Île de la Cité on foot
  • No summit ticket and no tower restaurant

Day 3

Père Lachaise, Rodin, or Versailles

  • Père Lachaise or Musée Rodin if you want a second, smaller stop
  • Versailles only as the RER half-day, with its own ticket
  • Fromagerie and a bottle, then the stairs home

Book before you go

  • Louvre or Orsay timed ticket — pick one
  • One 10th–11th dinner if you want Septime / Frenchie / Le Comptoir
  • Navigo if your days qualify, otherwise a carnet
  • Atlantic crossing 2–4 months out, midweek. Open-jaw (in Paris, out Rome) often beats two one-ways

Hidden costs

  • A tower-view surcharge on top of a 10th-arrondissement rate
  • Summit ticket plus a museum the same day
  • A Museum Pass used for a single entry
  • Versailles ticket and RER fare treated as a free add-on
  • Paris hotel quotes often already include TVA. Do not add Trip Plan’s 10% Europe assumption on top and call it the statutory rate

Skip this

  • A restaurant on the tower steps — Trocadéro is the view. The bakery is the breakfast.
  • Four museums in a day — One timed ticket. A pass wins at two or more.
  • A rental car — The Metro is the product. Stairs are the other one.

One museum, one booking

Top money-saving tips

  1. Money edge: See the tower from Trocadéro for free. Buy a Museum Pass only if you will enter two museums.
  2. Check Navigo day-of-week rules before you buy the week. A carnet is the fallback.
  3. Versailles does not share an afternoon with the Louvre.
  4. Trip Finder’s shoulder mid is about $200–350 before you convert. A view block is a surcharge on that band, not a different city.
  5. Breakfast at Crillon or Bristol is its own line. The bakery is the other breakfast.
