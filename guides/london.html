Who this is for

For

Not for

Getting around

Contactless. Never a car. Zone 1–2 lodging.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • Contactless daily cap on Tube / bus. A Visitor Oyster only if you like souvenirs
  • South Bank, Bloomsbury, or South Ken — one neighborhood, no car
  • LHR via Elizabeth Line or Piccadilly. LGW and STN are different ground math — price it

  • Dinner Indian or Turkish in Zone 2, not a West End prix fixe
  • Meal deal is allowed; a tourist-menu roast is not the plan

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Contactless daily cap. You almost never need a paper ticket. A rental car is a Congestion Charge you do not want.
  2. Premier Inn or Travelodge Zone 1–2 on Budget. Mayfair is Splurge — luxury is the room plus £8 pints.
  3. Bakery or Tesco breakfast. Borough or Maltby lunch. Zone 2 Indian or Turkish dinner, not a West End prix fixe every night.
  4. British Museum or National Gallery are free. The Eye is a pick-one with the Tower — not both on Budget.
  5. West End rush / day seats before a full-price orchestra. Splurge is one reserved seat, not three.
  6. Book the Atlantic 2–4 months out, midweek. Open-jaw with Paris or Dublin often beats two one-ways.
  7. Skip a paid Eye ticket on Budget — South Bank walk is the product.
  8. Hotel-included breakfast only if it is actually in the rate. Pret is a fallback, not a personality.
