Who this is for

For

  • People who will walk or bike Old Town and watch sunset from the sidewalk
  • Anyone who will skip Mallory menus and Stock Island lodging

Not for

  • Anyone stacking Tortugas, a sunset sail, and a seaplane in three nights
  • Fantasy Fest if you did not come for it

Getting around

Walk or bike Old Town. Skip a rental if you sleep there; a car helps for the Overseas Highway, not nightly Duval hops. Drive-down from Miami is a day you should price as a day.

No rental

  • Old Town is the product. Bike or walk. Duval is a street, not a hotel strategy
  • EYW is tiny and expensive air. Midweek, or accept the premium
  • Stock Island lodging only if you like the commute. Most people should not

With a rental

  • Drive-down from Miami is a full day — price it as a day, not a surprise
  • Old Town parking is a fee. Still one base. Use the car for the Overseas Highway, not nightly Duval hops
  • A cheap Stock Island room plus a nightly cab is not Budget

Where to eat

Cuban Coffee Queen or a ventanita. El Siboney or Garbo’s at night. Louie’s Backyard or Latitudes once. Kermit’s key lime once, not as dinner.

Things to do

Mallory Square from the sidewalk at sunset. Fort Zachary Taylor for the swim. The Dry Tortugas ferry only if it is the whole day. A Sebago sail does not share that day.

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Duval on foot

  • Cuban Coffee Queen
  • Walk Duval as a street, not a hotel strategy
  • Sunset from the Mallory sidewalk — no pier ticket

Day 2

El Siboney or Louie’s

  • El Siboney, Garbo’s, or Louie’s Backyard — one dinner
  • Kermit’s key lime once
  • Schooner Wharf fritters are a snack, not the meal

Day 3

Fort Zach, or the ferry

  • Fort Zachary Taylor for the beach and the fort
  • Or the Dry Tortugas ferry if that is the entire day
  • A Sebago sunset sail does not follow the ferry

Book before you go

  • Old Town room you can walk from
  • Dry Tortugas ferry only if that is the full day
  • Blue Heaven if you want that wait — go early
  • EYW midweek, or drive from Miami as a priced day

Hidden costs

  • Florida lodging tax about 12.5%
  • Old Town parking if you drove a car you will not use
  • Stock Island cab both ways
  • EYW airfare premium versus a priced drive-down day from Miami
  • A Tortugas ticket plus a sunset sail plus a seaplane

Skip this

  • A Mallory Square menu — The sunset is the same from the sidewalk.
  • Stock Island to save the room — The nightly cab spends the savings.
  • Ferry, sail, and seaplane — The ferry is a day. Pick one of the others, on a different day.

Walk Old Town, price the ferry

Top money-saving tips

  1. Money edge: Watch sunset from the Mallory sidewalk. The Dry Tortugas ferry is a different day, not an evening add-on.
  2. EYW air is a premium. A drive from Miami is a full day — put it on the plan as a day.
  3. Old Town parking is a fee for a car that should not be your Duval strategy.
  4. Fort Zachary Taylor is the cheap outdoor ticket. Parasail is optional.
  5. Blue Heaven at opening if you want that courtyard. Otherwise El Siboney, without the wait.
