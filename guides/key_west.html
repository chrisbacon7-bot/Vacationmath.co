Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Cuban Coffee Queen or a ventanita breakfast. Mallory Square menus are how you overpay.
  2. Sunset from the sidewalk. Skip a paid pier ticket on Lean — the sun does the same work.
  3. El Siboney or Garbo’s for Lean dinner. Blue Heaven early if you have room in the budget for the wait.
  4. Stay Old Town. A cheap Stock Island room plus a nightly cab is not Lean.
  5. Fort Zach beach + fort is the cheap outdoor ticket. Parasail only if you're spending more.
  6. Dry Tortugas is a full ferry day — only if you have room in the budget. Do not stack Tortugas, a sunset sail, and a seaplane in three nights.
  7. EYW air is a premium. Midweek, or drive from Miami as a priced day, not a surprise.
  8. Key lime pie once. Happy-hour conch fritters are a snack, not dinner.
