Who this is for

For

  • People who will walk or bike Old Town and watch sunset from the sidewalk
  • Anyone who will skip Mallory menus and Stock Island lodging

Not for

  • Anyone stacking Tortugas, a sunset sail, and a seaplane in three nights
  • Fantasy Fest if you did not come for it

Getting around

Walk Old Town. A rental car is a parking fee. Drive-down from Miami is a day you should price as a day.

No rental

  • Old Town is the product. Bike or walk. Duval is a street, not a hotel strategy
  • EYW is tiny and expensive air. Midweek, or accept the premium
  • Stock Island lodging only if you like the commute. Most people should not

With a rental

  • Drive-down from Miami is a full day — price it as a day, not a surprise
  • Old Town parking is a fee. Still one base. Use the car for the Overseas Highway, not nightly Duval hops
  • A cheap Stock Island room plus a nightly cab is not Budget

Where to stay

Old Town guesthouse you can walk from. Casa Marina is Splurge. Stock Island plus a cab is not Budget.

Budget

  • Hampton Inn Key West points-friendly Old Town-adjacent, walk or a short hop to Duval
  • Holiday Inn Express Key West points-friendly limited-service, skip an EYW motel if you will Uber downtown every meal
  • The Big Ruby Key West guesthouse, walk to Duval, no car

Mid-range

  • Hyatt Centric Key West Resort & Spa points-friendly Old Town water, walk to dinner
  • Courtyard Key West Waterfront points-friendly Marriott, one neighborhood
  • Hilton Garden Inn Key West points-friendly walkable mid if you already priced it
  • The Gardens Hotel Old Town courtyard if you want inn over a chain campus

Splurge

  • Casa Marina Key West, A Waldorf Astoria Resort points-friendly beach-adjacent historic, south of Duval
  • The Reach Key West, Curio Collection Waldorf-adjacent, walk to the sand

Where to eat

Cuban Coffee Queen or a ventanita. El Siboney or Garbo’s at night. Blue Heaven early if you already priced the wait.

Budget

  • Breakfast Cuban Coffee Queen or a ventanita, not the hotel
  • Lunch: Garbo’s Grill or Eaton Street Seafood counter, not Mallory
  • Dinner El Siboney Cuban, stay off Duval’s tourist row
  • Key lime pie once, not as a meal plan

Mid-range

  • Breakfast Cuban coffee + toastado
  • Lunch Pepe’s Café or a harbor casual
  • Dinner: Blue Heaven go early covers the wait
  • One Duval sit-down, not five

Splurge

  • Breakfast still a café
  • Lunch a proper sit-down off the square
  • Dinner reservation Louie’s Backyard or Hot Tin Roof Splurge
  • Nine One Five if you already priced it

Things to do

Sunset from the sidewalk. Fort Zach for the swim. Dry Tortugas is a full ferry day — only if you already priced it.

Free / cheap

  • Mallory sunset from the sidewalk skip a paid pier ticket on Budget (free)
  • Duval walk in daylight; the bar crawl is optional (free)
  • Southernmost Point is a photo, not a morning (free)

Paid — pick one

  • Fort Zach snorkel or a cheap boat pick one (ticketed)
  • Hemingway House timed ticket (ticketed)
  • Sunset sail Splurge if the sidewalk sunset was not enough (tour)

3-day skeleton

Day 1

The island you booked

  • Ventanita, walk Duval as a street, Fort Zach if you want a swim
  • Sunset from the sidewalk
  • No Mallory menu

Day 2

One named dinner

  • El Siboney or Garbo’s, or Blue Heaven if you already priced the wait
  • Key lime pie once
  • Happy-hour fritters are a snack

Day 3

More walking — Tortugas only if you already priced it

  • The same Old Town
  • Dry Tortugas is a full day
  • Do not add a seaplane and a sunset sail on the same card

Book before you go

  • Old Town room you can walk from
  • Dry Tortugas ferry only if that is the full day
  • Blue Heaven if you want that wait — go early
  • EYW midweek, or drive from Miami as a priced day

Hidden costs

  • Florida lodging tax about 12.5%
  • Old Town parking if you drove
  • Stock Island cab every night
  • EYW air premium
  • Mallory menu surcharges on the same sunset
  • Tortugas + sail + seaplane stacked into three nights

Skip this

  • Mallory Square menus — A tax on the same sunset you can watch from the sidewalk.
  • A cheap Stock Island room — Plus a nightly cab is not Budget.
  • Tortugas + a sunset sail + a seaplane — Do not stack three big days into three nights.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Cuban Coffee Queen or a ventanita breakfast. Mallory Square menus are a tax.
  2. Sunset from the sidewalk. Skip a paid pier ticket on Budget — the sun does the same work.
  3. El Siboney or Garbo’s for Budget dinner. Blue Heaven early if you already priced the wait.
  4. Stay Old Town. A cheap Stock Island room plus a nightly cab is not Budget.
  5. Fort Zach beach + fort is the cheap outdoor ticket. Parasail is optional.
  6. Dry Tortugas is a full ferry day — only if you already priced it. Do not stack Tortugas, a sunset sail, and a seaplane in three nights.
  7. EYW air is a premium. Midweek, or drive from Miami as a priced day, not a surprise.
  8. Key lime pie once. Happy-hour conch fritters are a snack, not dinner.
