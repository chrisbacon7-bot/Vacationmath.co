Maui vacation costs: stay, eat, get around (2026)

A Kihei condo with a kitchen already includes the beach in front of it. The expensive week adds Hana, a Haleakalā sunrise, and a Molokini boat as if they were errands.

Start here Take a Kihei kitchen — Aston Maui Kamaole, Kohea Kai, or something in that class — and grocery in the first hour after OGG. The sand that day is Keawakapu. The big outing gets its own morning.

Getting around

Kihei to Kaanapali is a drive. The car is the island the day you leave the condo path. Haleakalā before dawn needs that car and a separate sunrise reservation.

No rental

  • Possible only if you booked a walk-to-beach condo and will not leave that path
  • Most weeks should not try this — food trucks and Hana both assume a car
  • Airport shuttle plus a grocery delivery is the honest no-car version

With a rental

  • Pick up the car at OGG and grocery before the condo. The first hour is the budget
  • Kihei / Wailea / Kaanapali — pick one coast. Do not hotel-hop
  • Road to Hana is an early start, not a beach day. Haleakalā sunrise is a 2 a.m. ticket

Where to eat

Grocery at the first market. Kihei Caffe or Paia Fish Market for one plate. Mama’s Fish House or Spago once. Skip the Westin or Sheraton breakfast buffet.

Budget

  • Grocery at the first market after OGG
  • A Kihei or Paia food truck lunch
  • Cook two nights

Mid-range

  • Kihei Caffe one plate
  • Paia Fish Market on the way back from Hana
  • A Kaanapali casual the night you are already on that path
  • Monkeypod Kitchen if you are already in Wailea not a reason to change hotels

Splurge

  • Mama's Fish House in Paia book it or eat elsewhere
  • Spago at Four Seasons Wailea the other big dinner, not the same night
  • The Westin or Sheraton breakfast buffet is why the condo was cheaper
  • Morimoto Maui or Spago one Wailea dinner. Mama's Fish House is the Paia reservation, not the same night

Things to do

The beach in front of the condo is day one. Haleakalā sunrise is its own reservation and a 2 a.m. start. Road to Hana is a different day. Molokini is a third.

Budget

  • Keawakapu or the beach in front of the condo
  • A shore snorkel and a grocery picnic
  • Watch a later morning from a lot you can enter after 7 a.m. if you do not hold a sunrise reservation

Mid-range

  • Road to Hana as an early start, weather permitting its own day
  • Haleakalā sunrise only with the Recreation.gov reservation
  • Pride of Maui or Kai Kanani only if Molokini is the booked day

Splurge

  • Do not stack Hana, the summit, and a boat in four days
  • A second hotel so you can be closer to Mama's is a second lodging night
  • Hotel Wailea still means a drive to dinner
  • Kai Kanani leaves from the Wailea side; Pride of Maui is the Maalaea boat. They are different harbors

Book before you go

  • Haleakalā sunrise reservation if that is the 2 a.m. ticket
  • Hana as an early start, not a rushed Budget day
  • Car at OGG plus a grocery stop the first hour
  • One fish dinner if you want Mama’s — not five

Hidden costs

  • Hawaii lodging tax about 17.75% on a pre-tax quote
  • Resort fees and parking on Kaanapali and Wailea
  • A resort breakfast buffet
  • Haleakalā reservation on top of a boat on top of Hana
  • A second lodging night so you can be closer to one restaurant

Skip this

  • Hana, Haleakalā, and Molokini in four days — Pick one. The beach is the rest.
  • Road to Hana as a casual afternoon — It is an early start, and the weather can close it.
  • Resort breakfast as the habit — The kitchen is why the condo was cheaper.

The kitchen is the budget

Top money-saving tips

  1. Hidden gem: Haleakalā sunrise is a $1 vehicle reservation on Recreation.gov, required from 3 a.m. to 7 a.m., on top of the park entrance fee. A fee-free entrance day does not waive it, and there is no first-come line at the gate.
  2. Hidden gem: A Kihei kitchen at Aston Maui Kamaole or Kohea Kai is the budget. Sheraton or Westin Kaanapali still owes a resort fee and parking before that rate beats the condo.
  3. Hidden gem: Kai Kanani and Pride of Maui leave from different harbors. Booking 'a Molokini boat' without the harbor means a drive you did not put on the Hana day. Those are three mornings, not one.
  4. Grocery the first hour after OGG. Two cooked dinners change the week more than skipping Mama's.
  5. Paia Fish Market is the plate on the way back from Hana. It is not a reason to book a second hotel.
