Money guide · Maui, Hawaii

Maui vacation costs: stay, eat, get around (2026)

A Kihei condo with a kitchen already includes the beach in front of it. The expensive week treats the Road to Hana, a Haleakalā sunrise, and a Molokini boat as if they were the same morning.

Start here

Book a Kihei condo with a kitchen, such as Aston Maui Kamaole or Kohea Kai, or the Sheraton if you want the Kaanapali beach path. Grocery the first hour after the Kahului airport. Pick one big morning: the Road to Hana, a Haleakalā sunrise, or a Molokini boat.

Where to base yourself

  1. 01

    A Kihei condo with a kitchen

    Book Aston Maui Kamaole, Kohea Kai, or the Maui Coast Hotel in Kihei. Rooms are about $200–400 a night before Hawaii’s 17.75% lodging tax. You get a kitchen and a beach you can walk to. You do not get a resort path. Do not choose it if you will not cook and will buy a resort breakfast at about $30–50 a person.

  2. 02

    Kaanapali, on the beach path

    Book the Sheraton Maui, the Westin Maui, or Honua Kai. Rooms are about $400–650 a night before tax, plus a resort fee near $40 and parking about $25–40. You get the beach path. Do not choose it over a Kihei kitchen if you will cook and the lower rate still reaches a beach.

  3. 03

    Wailea

    Book Grand Wailea, about $700–1,200 a night before tax, plus a resort fee, or Andaz or the Four Seasons if the room is the treat. You get Wailea beach. You do not get every boat in the rate. Do not add a Molokini trip, about $150–200 a person, on top of this room unless that boat is the day.

  4. 04

    Kahului, the night you land

    Book the Holiday Inn Express Kahului, about $180–260 a night, only the night you land or fly. You get a bed near the airport. You do not get the beach. Do not spend the week there and drive to the sand every morning.

Who this is for

For

  • People who will sleep on one coast and cook most dinners in the condo

Not for

  • A short week that tries to hold Hana, Haleakalā, and a Molokini boat

Getting around

Most weeks need a car once you leave the beach in front of the condo. Kihei to Kaanapali is a drive. A compact rental in this example is about $70 a day before airport fees. You can skip the car only if you booked a walk-to-beach condo and will not leave that path.

No rental

  • The beach in front of a Kihei condo or a Kaanapali hotel does not need a car that day
  • A food truck in Kihei or Paia is a short hop, not a reason to change hotels
  • Mama’s Fish House is one dinner reservation. It does not require a second hotel

With a rental

  • A compact rental is about $60–90 a day before airport fees. This example uses about $70
  • Haleakalā and the Road to Hana are drives. They are not the same morning
  • Kaanapali and Wailea add parking, about $25–40 a night, on top of a resort fee near $40

Where to stay

Pay for a kitchen if you will cook. A Kihei condo such as Aston Maui Kamaole is about $250–400 a night before Hawaii’s 17.75% lodging tax. The Sheraton or the Westin on Kaanapali is about $400–650, plus a resort fee and parking. Grand Wailea is the splurge room, still on one coast.

Where to eat

Grocery breakfast is about $8–12 a person. A Kihei food truck is about $14–20. Mama’s Fish House is about $80–150 a person, one reservation. A resort breakfast buffet is about $30–50 a person, which is why the kitchen was the point.

Things to do

The beach in front of the condo is the first day. Haleakalā National Park is $30 a vehicle, plus a $1 sunrise reservation on Recreation.gov, required from 3 a.m. to 7 a.m. A Molokini boat is about $150–200 a person. The Road to Hana is a different morning.

3-day skeleton

Day 1

The beach you paid for

  • Grocery the first hour, about $40–70 for two.
  • The beach in front of the condo or the Kaanapali path. $0.
  • Cook dinner.

Day 2

One big morning

  • The Road to Hana as an early start, or Haleakalā sunrise as the $1 reservation plus the $30 park fee. One of them.
  • Home to the same coast.
  • Not a boat the same day.

Day 3

The sand, unless the boat is booked

  • The beach in front of the bed.
  • A Molokini boat, about $150–200 a person, only if that is the booked day.
  • Mama’s Fish House or Spago only as the one reservation.

Book before you go

  • The condo or the Kaanapali hotel. A Kihei kitchen is about $200–400 before the 17.75% tax. Add the resort fee before a beach-path rate wins.
  • The Haleakalā sunrise reservation, $1, from 3 a.m. to 7 a.m., on top of the $30 vehicle entrance.
  • The car at the Kahului airport, about $60–90 a day before fees, plus a grocery stop the first hour.
  • One fish dinner if you want Mama’s, about $80–150 a person. Not five.

Hidden costs

  • Hawaii lodging tax, about 17.75%, on a pre-tax quote.
  • Kaanapali and Wailea resort fees, near $40 a night, and self-parking about $25–40 more.
  • A resort breakfast buffet, about $30–50 a person.
  • Haleakalā is $30 a vehicle plus a $1 sunrise reservation. A Molokini boat is about $150–200 a person. A guided Hana van is about $150. Those are three mornings.
  • A second lodging night so you can be closer to one restaurant.

Skip this

  • Hana, Haleakalā, and Molokini in one short week — Pick one. The summit is $31 for the car. The boat is about $150–200 a person. The beach is the rest.
  • The Road to Hana as a casual afternoon — It is an early start, and the weather can close it. A guided van is about $150 a person.
  • Resort breakfast as the habit — The kitchen is why the Kihei condo was cheaper. The buffet is about $30–50 a person.

Cook in the condo, and pick one big morning.

Top money-saving tips

  1. Hidden gem: Book the Haleakalā sunrise as its own morning. It is a $1 reservation from 3 a.m. to 7 a.m., plus $30 for the vehicle. It is not a beach afternoon.
  2. Hidden gem: Add the resort fee, near $40, and parking, about $25–40, before a Kaanapali rate beats a Kihei kitchen.
  3. Hidden gem: Grocery the first hour. Two cooked nights change the week more than skipping the one fish dinner.
  4. A Paia food truck on the way back from Hana is about $12–18. It is not a reason to book a second hotel in Paia.
  5. Mama’s Fish House is one reservation, about $80–150 a person. Do not change hotels for it.
