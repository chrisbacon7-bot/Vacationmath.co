Maui

Who this is for

For

  • People who will pick Kihei, Kaanapali, or Wailea and stay there
  • Anyone who will grocery the first hour and cook two nights

Not for

  • Anyone stacking Hana, Haleakalā, and Molokini in four days
  • People who will hotel-hop coasts for one dinner

Getting around

A car is the island once you leave the condo path. Price parking and a grocery stop the first hour.

No rental

  • Possible only if you booked a walk-to-beach condo and will not leave that path
  • Most weeks should not try this — food trucks and Hana both assume a car
  • Airport shuttle plus a grocery delivery is the honest no-car version

With a rental

  • Pick up the car at OGG and grocery before the condo. The first hour is the budget
  • Kihei / Wailea / Kaanapali — pick one coast. Do not hotel-hop
  • Road to Hana is an early start, not a beach day. Haleakalā sunrise is a 2 a.m. ticket

Where to stay

Kihei condo first — walk to a food truck. Cook two nights. Kaanapali if you want the beach path. Pick one coast.

Budget

  • Holiday Inn Express Kahului points-friendly crash-pad only the night you fly; Budget lodging is a Kihei condo
  • Maui Coast Hotel Kihei, walk to a food truck, grocery the first hour

Mid-range

  • Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa points-friendly Kaanapali Beach, walk the path
  • The Westin Maui Resort & Spa points-friendly same beach, resort fees will show up
  • Hyatt Regency Maui Resort and Spa points-friendly Kaanapali, bigger campus
  • Wailea Ekahi or a Kihei-plus condo one neighborhood, not three islands

Splurge

  • Grand Wailea, A Waldorf Astoria Resort points-friendly Wailea Beach, the Splurge default
  • Andaz Maui at Wailea points-friendly Hyatt, same area, only if you're spending more
  • The Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua points-friendly west side, a different drive
  • Four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea only if you're spending more

Same names on Trip Plan — examples, not live inventory and not a ranking.

Where to eat

Grocery the first hour. Food trucks in Kihei or Paia. One fish dinner, not five. Resort breakfast buffets are a second lodging charge.

Budget

  • Breakfast grocery the condo
  • Lunch food truck in Kihei or Paia
  • Dinner cook two nights, one casual fish plate
  • Food truck in Kihei or Paia

Mid-range

  • Breakfast condo + one café
  • Lunch food truck or a plate lunch
  • Dinner one fish dinner, not five
  • Paia or Lahaina casual over the hotel dining room

Splurge

  • Breakfast condo still wins
  • Lunch a sit-down in Wailea only if you are already there
  • Dinner reservation Mama’s Fish House or a Wailea table only if you're spending more
  • One named reservation, then the kitchen

Things to do

The beach in front of the condo is the product. Pick one big outing on purpose.

Free / cheap

  • The beach in front of the condo that is the product (free)
  • Grocery picnic and a snorkel from shore (cheap)

Paid — pick one

  • One snorkel from shore or a cheap boat (ticketed / cheap)
  • Road to Hana only if you start early and do not treat it as a beach day (car)
  • Haleakalā sunrise is ticketed and a 2 a.m. wake-up pick it on purpose

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Condo, grocery, the beach in front

  • Cook tonight
  • Walk to a truck if you must go out
  • You already paid for this sand

Day 2

One big outing — or none

  • Hana or Haleakalā, not both
  • Rain makes Hana a different brochure
  • Come home to the same coast

Day 3

More beach. A boat only if you already priced it

  • Molokini is optional, not a default
  • Do not hotel-hop to Wailea for one dinner

Book before you go

  • Haleakalā sunrise reservation if that is the 2 a.m. ticket
  • Hana as an early start, not a rushed Budget day
  • Car at OGG plus a grocery stop the first hour
  • One fish dinner if you want Mama’s — not five

Hidden costs

  • Hawaii lodging tax about 17.8%
  • Resort fees and parking on Kaanapali / Wailea
  • Car for the week plus gas on Hana
  • Resort breakfast buffets as a habit
  • Every excursion stacked on the room

Skip this

  • Road to Hana as a rushed Budget day — Early start, not a beach day. Rain is a different brochure.
  • Hana + Haleakalā + Molokini in four days — Pick one. The beach you booked is the rest.
  • Resort breakfast buffets as a habit — A second lodging charge. The kitchen exists.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Grocery the condo the first hour. Cook two nights. Resort breakfast buffets are a second lodging charge.
  2. Food trucks in Kihei or Paia. One fish dinner, not five.
  3. The beach in front of the condo is the product. Skip Road to Hana as a rushed Budget day.
  4. Haleakalā sunrise is ticketed and a 2 a.m. wake-up — pick it on purpose, not as a default.
  5. Do not stack Hana, Haleakalā, and a Molokini boat in four days.
  6. Resort fees and parking will show up on Kaanapali / Wailea. Add them in your head.
  7. May and September are the value window. Winter holidays are a different island.
  8. Mama’s Fish House is Splurge. The kitchen plus one casual plate is Mid-range.
