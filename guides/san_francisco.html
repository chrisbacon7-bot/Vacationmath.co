Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Clipper + Muni / BART. A visitor passport beats a week of single rides if you move.
  2. Tartine or a Mission bakery breakfast. Skip the hotel dining room and the Wharf seafood rack.
  3. Alcatraz timed ferry — book ahead. Do not stack Alcatraz, Napa, and Yosemite in five nights.
  4. Skip the paid cable-car loop if a bus reaches the same hill. The souvenir ride is one ticket, not a commute.
  5. Napa is a day trip with a packed lunch, not a dinner transfer and a second hotel.
  6. Midweek SFO. Weekend rates around conventions and Fleet Week are a different city.
  7. Golden Gate + Crissy Field + Mission murals are free. SFMOMA is one ticketed indoor, not three.
  8. Union Square tourist hotels are a tax. Embarcadero or a neighborhood 3-star is the Mid-range stay.
