Money guide · San Francisco, California

San Francisco vacation costs: stay, eat, get around (2026)

A Clipper card and a Mission bakery will get you through San Francisco. A Union Square tourist hotel plus Fisherman's Wharf seafood on the same day will not.

Start here Hotel Emeline if you want to walk to the Ferry Building; the Mission if Tartine is the breakfast. Book the official Alcatraz ferry before you invent a second museum.

Who this is for

For

  • People who will tap Clipper and sleep near BART or the Embarcadero
  • Anyone who wants hills, a bakery, and one timed ferry

Not for

  • Anyone stacking Alcatraz, Napa, and Yosemite in five nights
  • People who want a rental car inside the city

Getting around

Clipper on Muni and BART is the system. The Powell-Hyde cable car is one souvenir fare on top of that, not the pass. Parking is a Napa or Muir Woods expense.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • Clipper card on Muni, BART, and ferries. A visitor passport can win on a four-ride day
  • Cable cars are a ticketed souvenir, not transit. A bus often reaches the same hill
  • SFO via BART. OAK is a different transit story — price the ground

Things to do

Crissy Field and the Embarcadero are free. Alcatraz is the timed ferry that sells out. A Powell-Hyde cable car is a souvenir fare, not your transit.

Mid-range

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Ferry Building and the water

  • Tartine or a Ferry Building breakfast
  • Embarcadero to Crissy Field
  • Dinner in the Mission or at Zuni — not the Wharf

Day 2

Alcatraz, then one museum

  • The official Alcatraz ferry, booked ahead
  • SFMOMA if you still want an indoor ticket
  • Not Napa the same afternoon

Day 3

Mission, or a Nob Hill bus

  • Mission murals and a taqueria
  • Powell-Hyde once if you want the souvenir ride, then a Muni bus
  • Skip a third museum

Book before you go

  • Alcatraz timed ferry — it sells out
  • Clipper or a visitor passport if you will ride
  • One dinner if you want State Bird or Zuni
  • SFO via BART; price OAK ground separately

Hidden costs

  • San Francisco lodging tax about 16%
  • Hotel parking on a Napa day
  • A cable-car souvenir fare on top of Clipper
  • Wharf seafood prices versus the Ferry Building
  • A rideshare over a hill the bus already climbs

Skip this

  • A Union Square tourist hotel — Same Muni ride as a Mission or Embarcadero bed, higher rate.
  • A Wharf seafood rack — The Ferry Building is the waterfront meal.
  • Alcatraz, Napa, and Yosemite — One timed ferry is the ticket. The others are different trips.

Clipper, then one ferry

Top money-saving tips

  1. Hidden gem: Ride the Muni bus up Nob Hill on Clipper. Buy the Powell-Hyde cable car once, as a souvenir fare, not as the pass that gets you around the city.
  2. Hidden gem: Book Alcatraz from the official ferry. It sells out. A booth at the Wharf is not the reservation system.
  3. Hidden gem: A Clipper visitor product wins on a four-ride day. Count tomorrow's rides before you buy it. One cable-car trip does not make the pass.
  4. Hotel parking is for a Napa day. The Mission, the Ferry Building, and Crissy Field do not need a car.
  5. State Bird Provisions or Quince is one dinner. Tartine is the next morning.
