Money guide · San Francisco, California

San Francisco

Transit-first city. Union Square tourist hotels are the expensive version of a Muni pass.

Who this is for

For

  • People who will tap Clipper and sleep near BART or the Embarcadero
  • Anyone who wants hills, a bakery, and one timed ferry

Not for

  • Anyone stacking Alcatraz, Napa, and Yosemite in five nights
  • People who want a rental car inside the city

Getting around

Muni + BART. Parking is a line item if you day-trip Napa or Muir Woods.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • Clipper card on Muni, BART, and ferries. A visitor passport can win on a four-ride day
  • Cable cars are a ticketed souvenir, not transit. A bus often reaches the same hill
  • SFO via BART. OAK is a different transit story — price the ground

Where to stay

Embarcadero or a neighborhood 3-star is the Mid-range stay. Hostel-plus near BART is Budget. A car inside the city is a parking line.

Budget

  • Hampton Inn San Francisco Downtown/Convention Center points-friendly walk to BART
  • Holiday Inn Express San Francisco Fisherman’s Wharf points-friendly only if that neighborhood is the trip
  • Courtyard San Francisco Downtown points-friendly Marriott value, Union Square-adjacent
  • Hotel Zephyr Wharf 2–3 star if you want that grid

Mid-range

  • Hilton San Francisco Union Square points-friendly cable-car adjacent, BART downstairs
  • San Francisco Marriott Marquis points-friendly Moscone / SoMa, one room not a suite
  • Hyatt Regency San Francisco points-friendly Embarcadero, Ferry Building walk
  • Hotel Emeline Jackson Square boutique if you want the neighborhood over a convention tower

Splurge

  • Fairmont San Francisco points-friendly Nob Hill, cable car at the door
  • St. Regis San Francisco points-friendly SoMa, walk to SFMOMA
  • JW Marriott San Francisco Union Square points-friendly Splurge without a palace rate
  • Four Seasons Hotel San Francisco SoMa flagship

Where to eat

Tartine or a Mission bakery. Ferry Building or a taqueria at lunch. Skip the Wharf seafood rack.

Budget

  • Breakfast Tartine or a Mission bakery, not the hotel
  • Lunch Ferry Building or a taqueria (La Taqueria / El Farolito class)
  • Dinner Mission or Chinatown, stay on BART / Muni
  • La Taqueria / El Farolito class Mission, stay on BART / Muni

Mid-range

  • Breakfast Tartine or a neighborhood café
  • Lunch Ferry Building oysters or a Mission burrito
  • Dinner one reservation (State Bird, Zuni, or a neighborhood Italian)
  • Napa is a day trip with a packed lunch, not a dinner transfer

Splurge

  • Breakfast a good café, still not the hotel dining room
  • Lunch Ferry Building or a proper sit-down
  • Dinner reservation: Atelier Crenn, Benu, or State Bird only if you're spending more
  • One tasting, then neighborhood food

Things to do

Golden Gate or Crissy Field, Embarcadero, Mission murals. Alcatraz is a timed ferry — book ahead.

Free / cheap

  • Golden Gate Bridge walk or Crissy Field (free)
  • Ferry Building + Embarcadero (free)
  • A neighborhood walk Mission murals or Chinatown (free)
  • Mission murals or Chinatown walk (free)

Paid — pick one

  • Alcatraz timed ferry book ahead (ticketed)
  • One museum SFMOMA or de Young (ticketed)
  • Napa is a day trip with a packed lunch, not a second hotel

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Embarcadero and a bakery

  • Ferry Building, the water, Tartine or a Mission stop if you rode that far
  • No Wharf prix fixe
  • Evening in the neighborhood you booked

Day 2

Alcatraz, or the bridge — not both plus Napa

  • Timed ferry if you booked it
  • Otherwise Golden Gate / Crissy Field and a neighborhood walk
  • Do not add Yosemite

Day 3

Mission murals or a packed-lunch day trip

  • Murals and a taqueria
  • Or Napa with a sandwich — not a dinner transfer and a second hotel
  • Home on BART

Book before you go

  • Alcatraz timed ferry — it sells out
  • Clipper or a visitor passport if you will ride
  • One dinner if you want State Bird or Zuni
  • SFO via BART; price OAK ground separately

Hidden costs

  • Hotel tax about 16%
  • Parking if you day-trip Napa or Muir Woods
  • Cable-car souvenir tickets used as transit
  • Wharf menu surcharges
  • BART from SFO vs a downtown taxi

Skip this

  • The Wharf seafood rack — Tourist menu, tourist price. The Mission is a bus away.
  • A paid cable-car loop as transit — One souvenir ticket is fine. A bus often reaches the same hill.
  • Napa for dinner — That is a transfer, not a reservation. Pack lunch or skip.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Clipper + Muni / BART. A visitor passport beats a week of single rides if you move.
  2. Tartine or a Mission bakery breakfast. Skip the hotel dining room and the Wharf seafood rack.
  3. Alcatraz timed ferry — book ahead. Do not stack Alcatraz, Napa, and Yosemite in five nights.
  4. Skip the paid cable-car loop if a bus reaches the same hill. The souvenir ride is one ticket, not a commute.
  5. Napa is a day trip with a packed lunch, not a dinner transfer and a second hotel.
  6. Midweek SFO. Weekend rates around conventions and Fleet Week are a different city.
  7. Golden Gate + Crissy Field + Mission murals are free. SFMOMA is one ticketed indoor, not three.
  8. Union Square tourist hotels are a tax. Embarcadero or a neighborhood 3-star is the Mid-range stay.
