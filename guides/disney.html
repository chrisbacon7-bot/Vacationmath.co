Disney World vacation costs: tickets, stay, eat (2026)

This is a park-ticket and lodging-grid trip. The overrun is Hopper plus the dining plan you should not buy.

Who this is for

For

  • Families who will stay on a bus, boat, or Skyliner grid and treat Magic Kingdom as a commute
  • Anyone who will grocery breakfast and skip the dining plan on purpose

Not for

  • People stacking four parks, a water park, and character dining before Thursday
  • Anyone calling an I-Drive hotel “Budget” without pricing parking and the drive

Getting around

Stay on property and the buses are the product. Skip a rental if you never leave Disney; I-Drive plus a car is a parking habit you bought on purpose.

No rental

  • On-property: bus to every park; Skyliner from Pop, Art of Animation, Caribbean Beach, Riviera
  • Monorail only from the Deluxe Magic Kingdom resorts
  • Mears or rideshare from MCO — Magical Express is gone. Price the transfer as its own line

With a rental

  • Park at the hotel every night — that is a line, not a surprise
  • Still one base. Use the car for a planned Springs or grocery run, not four park hops
  • Daily park parking is a ticket if you drive to the gates

Where to stay

Base on the Skyliner if you can. A cheap I-Drive hotel is not Budget — it is a parking habit.

Budget

  • Pop Century or Art of Animation Skyliner to Hollywood Studios and Epcot; food-court breakfast
  • All-Star Movies / Music / Sports cheapest on-property bus grid, longer Magic Kingdom commute

Mid-range

  • Caribbean Beach Skyliner hub; ask Riviera-side so the gondola is a walk
  • Port Orleans French Quarter boat to Disney Springs, still a bus to Magic Kingdom

Splurge

  • Grand Floridian / Contemporary / Polynesian monorail to Magic Kingdom
  • Beach Club or Yacht Club walk to Epcot; club level is a second nightly rate

Where to eat

The dining plan is usually a bad buy — even on Splurge. Grocery the room, mobile-order lunch, one table-service.

Budget

  • Garden Grocer or Winn-Dixie the night you land breakfast in the room
  • Cosmic Ray’s, Pecos Bill, or Satu’li Canteen mobile-order lunch, sit in the shade

Mid-range

  • Sci-Fi Dine-In or 50’s Prime Time one table-service, booked before you fly
  • Resort food-court dinner on the Skyliner night you already paid for the commute

Splurge

  • ‘Ohana or a character breakfast one morning you will not get back
  • California Grill or Space 220 Splurge reservation, not a nightly habit

Things to do

Hopper on a short trip usually costs more than a second park day. One park a day unless you will switch after lunch on purpose.

Budget

  • Magic Kingdom as one park day no Hopper
  • Disney Springs at night instead of a third park ticket

Mid-range

  • Hollywood Studios or Epcot on the Skyliner day
  • Animal Kingdom as the cheaper third park not a throwaway

Splurge

  • Lightning Lane Multi Pass on Magic Kingdom or Hollywood Studios in peak weeks only
  • Hopper only if you will switch after lunch a second park day is usually cheaper

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Magic Kingdom, then stop

  • Rope drop the land you actually care about
  • Food-court dinner back at the resort, or one mobile-order lunch
  • Springs or a Skyliner hop at night — not a Hopper dash to Epcot fireworks

Day 2

Skyliner park

  • Hollywood Studios or Epcot from Pop, Art of Animation, Caribbean Beach, or Riviera
  • Lightning Lane Multi Pass on a peak Magic Kingdom or Hollywood Studios day — about $16–32, not a default
  • Evening on the line or a resort food court

Day 3

Cheaper park or a rest day

  • Animal Kingdom, a second single-park ticket, or Springs plus a pool
  • Do not Hopper unless you will switch after lunch on purpose
  • Memory Maker and the refillable mug only if you will use them every day

Book before you go

  • Park tickets and the lodging grid before you fly
  • Lightning Lane Multi Pass on Magic Kingdom or Hollywood Studios in a peak week — about $16–32 a person
  • One table-service if you want it — California Grill / Space 220 / character breakfast sell out
  • MCO transfer (Mears or rideshare). Magical Express is gone

Hidden costs

  • Florida 12.5% lodging tax on the room
  • 6.5% sales tax on tickets
  • Daily park parking if you drive
  • MCO transfer — no Magical Express
  • Lightning Lane and Memory Maker if you tap yes at the gate
  • Tips on table-service and the refillable mug you will not finish

Skip this

  • The dining plan — Pay as you go. Even Splurge usually loses this bet.
  • Park Hopper on a 3-night — A second park day is cheaper than paying extra for Hopper unless you will switch after lunch.
  • I-Drive hotel plus a rental as “Budget” — You bought parking, gas, and a 40-minute commute.

Grid first, tickets second

Top money-saving tips

  1. Skip Hopper on four nights or fewer unless you switch parks after lunch. The Hopper add-on in the calculator runs about $65–105 per ticket.
  2. Lightning Lane Multi Pass is about $16–32 a person per day. It can earn its keep on Magic Kingdom or Hollywood Studios in peak weeks, and it is a skip in late January and mid-September.
  3. Grocery breakfast plus a food court beats the dining plan. Typical dining in the calculator is about $215 for a party of four; a heavy signature day runs about $320.
  4. Driving to the gate adds about $35 a day in park parking. On-property bus and Skyliner guests do not pay that line.
  5. Florida lodging tax is about 12.5% on the room (All-Star resorts in Osceola County are 13.5%), and ticket sales tax is 6.5% on top. Run Trip Plan before you lock.
