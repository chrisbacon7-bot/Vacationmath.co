Top money-saving tips

  1. Independence Hall timed entry is the ticket — book it. The Bell is a line, not a morning.
  2. Reading Terminal breakfast and lunch. Hotel restaurants are the expensive version of the same plate.
  3. One cheesesteak, then stop. Roast pork (DiNic’s class) is the local argument.
  4. SEPTA day pass beats a rideshare loop. Skip the hop-on bus on Lean.
  5. Barnes or PMA — pick one on Solid. Do not stack three interiors and a cheesesteak tour in one day.
  6. Midweek PHL. Fourth of July week is a different city and a different room.
  7. Stay Center City or Old City. An airport hotel is a SEPTA tax you will resent.
  8. Zahav is leftover. Terminal leftovers plus one neighborhood Italian is Solid.
