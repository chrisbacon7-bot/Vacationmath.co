Who this is for

For

  • Anyone who wants Terminal lunch and one museum

Not for

  • Anyone booking the airport hotel and calling it Center City
  • Fourth of July week if you came for the rate

Getting around

Walk Independence. Ride SEPTA for the rest. An airport hotel is a SEPTA commute you will resent every morning.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • SEPTA Key / Independence Pass if you will ride more than twice a day
  • Old City and Center City are walkable to each other if you like walking
  • PHL is a train, not a taxi habit. Price Regional Rail vs rideshare

Where to stay

Center City or Old City. Walk to Independence. The hotel tax is about 15.5% whether you slept next to a Cinnabon or not.

Budget

  • Club Quarters or a compact 2-star near City Hall walk to a Market-Frankford stop
  • Home2 or Hampton Center City grocery downstairs, not an airport hotel

Mid-range

  • The Notary Hotel City Hall area, walk to Reading Terminal
  • Kimpton Hotel Monaco Independence Mall walking

Splurge

  • Four Seasons Philadelphia Logan Square if you already priced it
  • The Rittenhouse square-adjacent; Independence timed entry is still the ticket

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Independence Hall timed entry is the ticket — book it. The Bell is a line, not a morning.
  2. Reading Terminal breakfast and lunch. Hotel restaurants are the expensive version of the same plate.
  3. One cheesesteak, then stop. Roast pork (DiNic’s class) is the local argument.
  4. SEPTA day pass beats a rideshare loop. Skip the hop-on bus on Budget.
  5. Barnes or PMA — pick one on Mid-range. Do not stack three interiors and a cheesesteak tour in one day.
  6. Midweek PHL. Fourth of July week is a different city and a different room.
  7. Stay Center City or Old City. An airport hotel is a SEPTA commute you will resent.
  8. Zahav is Splurge. Terminal plus one neighborhood Italian is Mid-range.
