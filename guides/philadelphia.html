Money guide · Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Philadelphia

Reading Terminal is the cheap-rich lunch. Cheesesteak is one meal, not a pilgrimage.

Quick facts

Nights that fit
2–3 nights
Mid-range posture
Center City + Reading Terminal + one museum
Best months
February, early March, late January after the holiday hangover
Need a car?
No

Who this is for

For

  • People who will walk Independence and ride SEPTA to the rest
  • Anyone who wants Terminal lunch and one museum

Not for

  • Anyone booking the airport hotel and calling it Center City
  • Fourth of July week if you came for the rate

Getting around

Walk Independence. Ride the rest. An airport hotel is a SEPTA tax you will resent.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • SEPTA Key / Independence Pass if you will ride more than twice a day
  • Old City and Center City are walkable to each other if you like walking
  • PHL is a train, not a taxi habit. Price Regional Rail vs rideshare

Where to stay

Center City or Old City. Walk to Independence. The hotel tax is about 15.5% whether you slept next to a Cinnabon or not.

Budget

  • Hampton Inn Philadelphia Center City-Convention Center points-friendly limited-service, grocery downstairs
  • Holiday Inn Express Philadelphia – Midtown points-friendly walk to a Broad Street Line stop
  • Courtyard Philadelphia Downtown points-friendly Marriott value near City Hall
  • Home2 Suites Philadelphia Downtown kitchenette if you will grocery breakfasts

Mid-range

  • Philadelphia Marriott Downtown points-friendly Convention Center, walk to Reading Terminal
  • Canopy by Hilton Philadelphia Center City points-friendly one neighborhood, not University City
  • Kimpton Hotel Monaco Philadelphia points-friendly Independence Mall walking
  • The Notary Hotel Hilton, City Hall neighborhood

Splurge

  • Four Seasons Hotel Philadelphia Logan Square Splurge

Where to eat

Reading Terminal for breakfast and lunch. One cheesesteak, then roast pork at DiNic’s class.

Budget

  • Breakfast Reading Terminal bakery or a Center City café, not the hotel
  • Lunch: Terminal stalls roast pork at DiNic’s class, or a market plate
  • Dinner one cheesesteak (Pat’s / Geno’s tourist, or a neighborhood shop) then stop
  • Italian Market if you are already in South Philly

Mid-range

  • Breakfast café most mornings
  • Lunch Reading Terminal or a proper casual
  • Dinner: one reservation Zahav Splurge-adjacent, or a neighborhood Italian
  • Cheesesteak once; roast pork is the local argument

Splurge

  • Breakfast still a café
  • Lunch a sit-down on the Parkway or Old City
  • Dinner reservation Zahav or a named tasting Splurge only
  • One splurge, then Terminal leftovers

Things to do

Independence Hall timed — book it. Barnes or PMA — pick one.

Free / cheap

  • Independence Hall timed entry + Liberty Bell (free / timed)
  • Reading Terminal morning (free / cheap)
  • Love Park / City Hall walk-through, not a hop-on bus (free)

Paid — pick one

  • Eastern State Penitentiary if you already priced a half day (ticketed)
  • Franklin Institute only if the party is kids-first (ticketed)
  • SEPTA day pass beats a rideshare loop

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Independence and the Terminal

  • Timed Hall, the Bell as a line you already expected
  • Lunch under one roof
  • Evening in Old City or Center City — you can walk it

Day 2

One museum

  • Barnes or PMA. Not both
  • Roast pork if you did the cheesesteak yesterday
  • No hop-on bus

Day 3

A neighborhood dinner, then the train home

  • Italian in the neighborhood, not Zahav unless you already priced it
  • PHL is Regional Rail, not a taxi habit

Book before you go

  • Independence Hall timed entry
  • Barnes or PMA — pick one
  • Zahav only if that is the dinner you came for
  • SEPTA Key if you will ride

Hidden costs

  • Combined lodging tax about 15.5%
  • Airport hotel plus SEPTA every morning
  • Hop-on bus you do not need
  • Cheesesteak tour stacked on three interiors
  • PHL taxi vs Regional Rail

Skip this

  • An airport hotel — A SEPTA tax you will resent every morning.
  • Cheesesteak as a pilgrimage — One, then roast pork. You have done the bit.
  • Three interiors and a tour bus — Independence + one museum. Hop-on is Budget’s enemy.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Independence Hall timed entry is the ticket — book it. The Bell is a line, not a morning.
  2. Reading Terminal breakfast and lunch. Hotel restaurants are the expensive version of the same plate.
  3. One cheesesteak, then stop. Roast pork (DiNic’s class) is the local argument.
  4. SEPTA day pass beats a rideshare loop. Skip the hop-on bus on Budget.
  5. Barnes or PMA — pick one on Mid-range. Do not stack three interiors and a cheesesteak tour in one day.
  6. Midweek PHL. Fourth of July week is a different city and a different room.
  7. Stay Center City or Old City. An airport hotel is a SEPTA tax you will resent.
  8. Zahav is Splurge. Terminal plus one neighborhood Italian is Mid-range.
