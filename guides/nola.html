Who this is for

For

  • People who came for Galatoire’s or Commander’s, not a balcony that photographs like a brochure
  • Anyone who will walk or take the St. Charles car

Not for

  • Mardi Gras “by accident” — it is a different budget
  • Anyone who wants a rental car as the default in Old Town

Getting around

Walk or streetcar. A rental car is a parking fee in Old Town.

No rental

  • Streetcar on Canal and St. Charles — a day pass if you will ride
  • Walk the Quarter in daylight; rideshare at night if you stray
  • MSY is a short ride

With a rental

  • Old Town parking is a fee you will resent
  • Still one base. Use the car for a planned plantation or swamp day, not dinner on Bourbon
  • Most food weeks never need it

Where to stay

Warehouse District, Mid-City, or Garden District. Visit the Quarter; do not necessarily pay to wake up in it.

Budget

  • Hampton Inn & Suites New Orleans Convention Center points-friendly Warehouse District, streetcar
  • Holiday Inn New Orleans – Downtown Superdome points-friendly limited-service, skip a Bourbon balcony on Budget
  • Courtyard New Orleans Downtown/Convention Center points-friendly Marriott value
  • HI New Orleans hostel-plus, streetcar not a Bourbon balcony

Mid-range

  • New Orleans Marriott points-friendly Canal, walk or streetcar
  • Hilton New Orleans Riverside points-friendly Convention Center mid
  • The Westin New Orleans points-friendly Canal mid
  • Omni Royal Orleans Quarter if you accept the premium

Splurge

  • The Roosevelt New Orleans, A Waldorf Astoria Hotel points-friendly Splurge
  • Windsor Court Hotel CBD Splurge

Where to eat

Café du Monde once. Then a neighborhood café. Po’boy lunch, one old-school dinner.

Budget

  • Breakfast café au lait + beignet once (Café du Monde), then a neighborhood spot
  • Lunch po’boy (Parkway or a neighborhood shop), not a Bourbon breakfast
  • Dinner casual Creole, not a tourist-menu courtyard every night
  • Po’boy (Parkway or a neighborhood shop), not a Bourbon breakfast

Mid-range

  • Breakfast neighborhood café
  • Lunch po’boy or gumbo
  • Dinner one old-school reservation (Galatoire’s or Commander’s Palace class)
  • Café du Monde once

Splurge

  • Breakfast café
  • Lunch a proper sit-down
  • Dinner reservation Commander’s, Galatoire’s, or a modern tasting Splurge
  • Keep the reservations; cut something else

Things to do

Daylight Quarter walks and a streetcar hour. Haunted tours are optional.

Free / cheap

  • A French Quarter walk in daylight (free)
  • A streetcar hour (cheap)

Paid — pick one

  • One house or a plantation pick one (ticketed)
  • A music ticket Splurge
  • A streetcar still

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Streetcar and a po’boy

  • Neighborhood café, Parkway or a shop lunch
  • Daylight Quarter
  • Café du Monde once if you must — not Bourbon breakfast

Day 2

The reservation you came for

  • Galatoire’s or Commander’s if that is the trip
  • Garden District walk or a streetcar hour before
  • Do not add a haunted tour on the same night

Day 3

Music, then stop stacking festivals

  • A club or a street you can walk
  • Jazz Fest and Mardi Gras are priced like what they are
  • Do not “just happen” to overlap them

Book before you go

  • Galatoire’s or Commander’s if that is the trip — book before you fly
  • Streetcar day pass if you will ride Canal and St. Charles
  • Flexible fare in hurricane season
  • Skip Mardi Gras lodging unless that is the trip

Hidden costs

  • Lodging tax about 16.2%
  • French Quarter room premium that is not the plate
  • Parking if you rent a car in Old Town
  • Haunted-tour upsells
  • Mardi Gras surge if you overlapped it by accident
  • Hurricane-season change fees

Skip this

  • French Quarter room premium as a default — The plate is the point. The balcony is a surcharge.
  • Bourbon breakfast every morning — Café du Monde once. Then walk.
  • Mardi Gras “by accident” — It is a different budget. Book it on purpose or miss it on purpose.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Cut the hotel class before you cut Galatoire’s or Commander’s. This is a food trip.
  2. Warehouse District, Mid-City, or Garden District. French Quarter room premium is real and not the plate.
  3. Café du Monde once. Then a neighborhood café — not a Bourbon breakfast every morning.
  4. Po’boy lunch (Parkway or a neighborhood shop). One old-school dinner, not a tourist-menu courtyard every night.
  5. Streetcar day pass. A rental car is a parking fee you will resent.
  6. Mardi Gras is a different budget — rooms, throw-away clothes, and surge. Do not “just happen” to overlap it.
  7. Hurricane-season weeks need a cancel stance you already priced. The music will still be there in January.
  8. Haunted tours are optional. Daylight Quarter walks and a streetcar hour are Budget.
