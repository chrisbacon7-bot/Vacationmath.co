Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Sleep a few blocks off Ocean Drive. Same sand, less neon, better breakfast.
  2. Cuban café (Versailles or a ventanita) — not the hotel, not Ocean Drive.
  3. Skip beach-club bottle service on Lean. That is a day-price, not a snack.
  4. Midweek flights. Friday into a holiday weekend is Miami doing Miami to your card.
  5. Wynwood Walls from the sidewalk; Little Havana on foot. Everglades is a half-day leftover, not a default.
  6. Hurricane-season “deals” need a flexible fare or a cancel-for-any-reason stance you already priced.
  7. Brickell if you want mainland restaurants and Metromover. Do not split Mid-Beach and Brickell in three nights.
  8. Joe’s Stone Crab is a share leftover — not five fish dinners.
