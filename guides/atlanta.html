Trail first, cloverleaf never

MARTA from ATL, then Midtown or Ponce so the BeltLine Eastside is the evening. Downtown convention hotels are a calendar tax. Cumberland / Buckhead cloverleaf lodging on Lean is how you buy parking and a steakhouse you did not need. Check the convention calendar before you celebrate a “deal.”

Base yourself here

If you cannot walk the Eastside Trail, you booked a parking garage.

Lean: Ponce or Midtown 3-star on MARTA / the BeltLine.

Stretch: Four Seasons Midtown or a nicer Midtown address — still not Cumberland.

Check the convention calendar first

Atlanta prices like a convention city. Shoulder late winter is the value window.

Go Late January through March, and November before Thanksgiving if you want walking weather without Peachtree-festival rates.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. MARTA from the airport. A cheap ATL-adjacent hotel is a rideshare habit.
  2. BeltLine + Ponce City Market is Lean entertainment. The Aquarium is one ticketed morning, not the whole trip.
  3. West Egg or a café breakfast. Mary Mac’s for a meat-and-three — skip a Buckhead steakhouse on Lean.
  4. Convention weeks are not the value window. Check the calendar before you book Downtown.
  5. MARTA / BeltLine day beats a rideshare loop. Do not split Buckhead and the Aquarium hotel in three nights.
  6. Midweek ATL. You already live near a hub if you flew Delta — midweek still wins.
  7. Piedmont Park and MLK National Historical Park are free / cheap. World of Coca-Cola is a pick-one with the Aquarium.
  8. Hotel Clermont or a Ponce 3-star if you want the trail. Four Seasons Midtown is leftover.
