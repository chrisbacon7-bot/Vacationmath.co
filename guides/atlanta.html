Who this is for

For

  • People who will ride MARTA from ATL and walk the BeltLine Eastside
  • Anyone who wants trail food and one ticketed morning

Not for

  • Convention weeks if you came for the rate
  • Anyone splitting Buckhead and the Aquarium hotel in three nights

Getting around

MARTA from ATL. Trail the rest. A rental is a parking fee if you stay Midtown and eat on the trail.

No rental

  • MARTA from ATL is the honest airport move. A 1-day or 3-day pass if you will ride
  • BeltLine Eastside Trail is the walk — Ponce City Market to Krog Street
  • You do not need a car for Midtown + Ponce + the Aquarium

With a rental

  • Cumberland / Buckhead cloverleaf lodging on Budget is how you buy parking
  • Still one base on the trail. Use the car for a planned day, not dinner two towers away
  • Check the convention calendar before you celebrate a downtown “deal”

Where to stay

Midtown or Ponce — Hampton, Hotel Clermont, or a 3-star on MARTA so the BeltLine Eastside is the evening.

Budget

  • Hampton Inn & Suites Atlanta-Midtown points-friendly limited-service, MARTA or BeltLine walking
  • Holiday Inn Express Atlanta Downtown points-friendly Centennial area, grocery in walking distance
  • Courtyard Atlanta Downtown points-friendly Marriott value, walk to a MARTA stop
  • Home2 Suites Atlanta Downtown kitchenette, skip a Cumberland cloverleaf

Mid-range

  • Atlanta Marriott Marquis points-friendly Downtown atrium, one room not a suite
  • Hyatt Regency Atlanta points-friendly Downtown, Peachtree walking
  • Hilton Atlanta points-friendly Downtown convention campus
  • Hotel Clermont Ponce boutique if you want BeltLine and Ponce City Market walking

Splurge

  • St. Regis Atlanta points-friendly Buckhead Splurge; you traded BeltLine walking
  • Grand Hyatt Atlanta in Buckhead points-friendly same area, only if you're spending more
  • JW Marriott Atlanta Buckhead points-friendly Splurge if you have room in the budget is Buckhead, not the Aquarium hotel
  • Four Seasons Hotel Atlanta Midtown only if you're spending more

Where to eat

West Egg or a café breakfast. Mary Mac’s for a meat-and-three. Skip a Buckhead steakhouse on Budget.

Budget

  • Breakfast West Egg or a Midtown café, not the hotel
  • Lunch Ponce City Market food hall or a BeltLine counter
  • Dinner: Mary Mac’s Tea Room meat-and-three, the Budget classic
  • Varasano’s or a neighborhood pizza if you skip the tourist row

Mid-range

  • Breakfast café most mornings
  • Lunch Ponce City Market or Krog Street Market
  • Dinner: Fox Bros. Bar-B-Q or a BeltLine sit-down one reservation
  • Mary Mac’s still wins one night

Splurge

  • Breakfast café
  • Lunch a sit-down in Midtown
  • Dinner reservation a named Midtown or Westside tasting only if you're spending more
  • One splurge, then a meat-and-three

Things to do

BeltLine Eastside — Ponce City Market to Krog Street. Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola — pick one.

Free / cheap

  • BeltLine Eastside Trail + Ponce City Market (free / cheap)
  • Piedmont Park (free)
  • MLK National Historical Park (free / cheap)

Paid — pick one

  • Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola pick one (ticketed)
  • Atlanta History Center or a Fox Theatre tour only if you're spending more (ticketed)
  • MARTA day pass beats a rideshare loop

3-day skeleton

Day 1

BeltLine and the neighborhood you booked

  • Café breakfast, Ponce City Market to Krog, dinner on the trail
  • You do not need a car for this day

Day 2

One ticketed morning

  • Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola — pick one
  • Piedmont or MLK the rest
  • Do not split Buckhead and Downtown hotels

Day 3

More trail, or go home

  • The same walk
  • Convention weeks are a different city
  • Four Seasons is Splurge, not a third neighborhood

Book before you go

  • MARTA from ATL — skip the airport hotel
  • Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola — pick one
  • Check the convention calendar before Downtown
  • Hotel Clermont or a Ponce 3-star if you want the trail

Hidden costs

  • Combined lodging tax about 16.9%
  • Airport-hotel rideshare twice a day
  • Buckhead parking and a steakhouse you did not need
  • Convention-week room surge
  • Second ticketed morning you stacked on the Aquarium

Skip this

  • A cheap ATL-adjacent hotel — You will Uber downtown twice a day. MARTA exists.
  • Splitting Buckhead and the Aquarium hotel — Three nights, two towers, one tired card.
  • Convention weeks — Not the value window. Read the calendar.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. MARTA from the airport. A cheap ATL-adjacent hotel is a rideshare habit.
  2. BeltLine + Ponce City Market is Budget entertainment. The Aquarium is one ticketed morning, not the whole trip.
  3. West Egg or a café breakfast. Mary Mac’s for a meat-and-three — skip a Buckhead steakhouse on Budget.
  4. Convention weeks are not the value window. Check the calendar before you book Downtown.
  5. MARTA / BeltLine day beats a rideshare loop. Do not split Buckhead and the Aquarium hotel in three nights.
  6. Midweek ATL. You already live near a hub if you flew Delta — midweek still wins.
  7. Piedmont Park and MLK National Historical Park are free / cheap. World of Coca-Cola is a pick-one with the Aquarium.
  8. Hotel Clermont or a Ponce 3-star if you want the trail. Four Seasons Midtown is Splurge.
