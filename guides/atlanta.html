Atlanta vacation costs: stay, eat, get around (2026)

MARTA from the airport and the BeltLine are the trip. A cheap hotel off the train is an Uber you will take twice a day.

Start here MARTA from ATL to Midtown or to Hotel Clermont on Ponce. Mary Mac’s for a meat-and-three. Georgia Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola — one morning.

Where to base yourself

  1. 1

    Midtown

    Hampton or the Georgian Terrace. MARTA and Piedmont. About $180–280 before 16.9% tax

  2. 2

    Ponce / Old Fourth

    Hotel Clermont. Ponce City Market and the Eastside Trail

  3. 3

    Downtown, convention week

    Only if the calendar is quiet. Centennial rates are not the normal week

  4. 4

    ATL-adjacent

    A lower room and an Uber downtown twice a day. MARTA already leaves the airport

Who this is for

For

  • People who will ride MARTA from ATL and walk the BeltLine Eastside
  • Anyone who wants trail food and one ticketed morning

Not for

  • Convention weeks if you came for the rate
  • Anyone splitting Buckhead and the Aquarium hotel in three nights

Getting around

MARTA from ATL. Trail the rest. Skip a rental if you stay Midtown and eat on the BeltLine; a car helps for Buckhead or a planned day, not dinner two towers away.

No rental

  • MARTA from ATL is the honest airport move. A 1-day or 3-day pass if you will ride
  • BeltLine Eastside Trail is the walk — Ponce City Market to Krog Street
  • You do not need a car for Midtown + Ponce + the Aquarium

With a rental

  • Cumberland / Buckhead cloverleaf lodging on Budget is how you buy parking
  • Still one base on the trail. Use the car for a planned day, not dinner two towers away
  • Check the convention calendar before you celebrate a downtown “deal”

Where to stay

Hampton or Home2 in Midtown, or Hotel Clermont on Ponce, so the BeltLine is the evening. Four Seasons is the Midtown flagship. St. Regis Buckhead trades the trail for a different fare.

Budget

  • Hampton or Home2 Midtown MARTA and grocery in walking distance
  • HI Atlanta or a Downtown hostel-plus walk to a MARTA stop, skip Cumberland

Mid-range

  • Hotel Clermont Ponce, walk to the BeltLine and Ponce City Market
  • The Georgian Terrace Midtown, Fox Theatre area

Splurge

  • Four Seasons Atlanta Midtown flagship; you still walk the BeltLine
  • St. Regis Atlanta Buckhead Splurge; you traded BeltLine walking

Where to eat

West Egg or a café, then Mary Mac’s. Fox Bros. Bar-B-Q if you are on the trail. Staplehouse or Bacchanalia once. Bones is a Buckhead ride.

Budget

  • West Egg or a Midtown café not the hotel
  • Mary Mac’s Tea Room meat-and-three, the Budget classic

Mid-range

  • Ponce City Market or Krog Street Market for lunch
  • Fox Bros. Bar-B-Q or a BeltLine sit-down one reservation

Splurge

  • Staplehouse or Bacchanalia one Westside dinner — then Mary Mac’s
  • Bones or a Buckhead steakhouse is a second fare from the BeltLine

Things to do

Walk Ponce City Market to Krog Street Market. Pay for the Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola, not both, unless the mornings are on different days and the nights are long enough.

Budget

  • BeltLine Eastside Ponce City Market to Krog Street
  • Piedmont Park or MLK National Historical Park

Mid-range

  • Georgia Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola pick one morning
  • MARTA day pass; do not split Buckhead and the Aquarium hotel

Splurge

  • Georgia Aquarium and a Fox Theatre tour on different mornings pick one if the nights are short
  • A Braves night is a ticket, not a third neighborhood

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Eastside Trail

  • Café breakfast in Midtown
  • Ponce City Market onto the BeltLine
  • Dinner on the trail, not in Buckhead

Day 2

One ticketed morning

  • Georgia Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola
  • Piedmont Park or the MLK National Historical Park after
  • Back on MARTA. Do not change hotels

Day 3

Inman Park to Krog

  • Eastside Trail from Ponce City Market through Inman Park to Krog Street Market
  • Fox Bros. or Mary Mac’s — one plate
  • A Fox Theatre tour only if the Aquarium morning is already behind you

Book before you go

  • MARTA from ATL — skip the airport hotel
  • Aquarium or World of Coca-Cola — pick one
  • Check the convention calendar before Downtown
  • Hotel Clermont or a Ponce 3-star if you want the trail

Hidden costs

  • Atlanta lodging tax about 16.9%
  • Two Ubers a day from an off-train hotel
  • A second ticketed morning stacked on the Aquarium
  • Convention-week Downtown rates
  • A Buckhead rideshare for a steakhouse

Skip this

  • A hotel that is not on MARTA — You will pay for the rides the train already runs.
  • Aquarium and World of Coca-Cola the same morning — Pick one.
  • Bones on a trail week — Mary Mac’s and Fox Bros. are the plates.

MARTA in, trail after

Top money-saving tips

  1. Money edge: MARTA from ATL to Midtown is one fare. The BeltLine from Ponce City Market to Krog does not charge admission.
  2. Read the convention calendar before you treat a Downtown rate as normal.
  3. The Aquarium and World of Coca-Cola are two tickets. The trail is not a third.
  4. Fox Bros. or Mary Mac’s. A Buckhead steakhouse adds a ride the trail did not require.
  5. Piedmont Park and the MLK National Historical Park do not sell a ticket. They are the afternoon after one morning admission.
