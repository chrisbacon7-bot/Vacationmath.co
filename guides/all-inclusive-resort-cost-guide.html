Read before you book

"All-inclusive" doesn't mean everything is included.

✓

What IS included

Meals, unlimited drinks (including alcohol), pools, beach, non-motorized water sports, and resort entertainment. This is the core of the rate and it represents genuine value — especially on the drinks side.

✗

What is NOT included

Airport transfers, motorized water sports, spa, scuba diving, off-resort excursions, and tips. Even at "tips included" resorts, guests still tip. Budget $400–700 on top of the resort rate for these line items.

✉

The airport transfer blind spot

Cancun airport to your resort is $25–60 one way for a shared shuttle, $80–150 each way for private. Most resorts don't include this. For a party of 4 with luggage, private is the practical choice — which means $160–300 round trip before you've even checked in.

✈

Destination matters

Mexico, Dominican Republic, and Jamaica have meaningfully different price points, flight availability, and resort quality tiers. The best "all-inclusive deal" depends heavily on where you're flying from and what trade-offs you're willing to make.

Destination comparison

Three destinations. Three different math problems.

🌴

Mexico — Cancun / Riviera Maya

Most US tourists go here for a reason. The largest selection of all-inclusive resorts in the world. Easiest flights from most US cities. Quality ranges from $150/night budget properties to $600+/night adults-only luxury. The catch: some older resorts are showing their age. Check recent reviews, not just star ratings.

🌊

Dominican Republic — Punta Cana

Strong value. Bavaro Beach is consistently excellent. Flight prices from the US are competitive. More turquoise water than Cancun. The risk: hurricane season runs June through November. Late summer and fall are peak risk windows. Travel insurance is more important here than anywhere else.

🏋

Jamaica — Montego Bay / Negril

More character, more vibrant. Airport transfer to Negril is 90 minutes — the drive matters. Montego Bay resorts cut that to 20 minutes. Jamaica tends to run $50–100/night higher than comparable Mexico or DR properties. Worth it if the culture and scenery are part of the point.

Making it work

Four moves that actually reduce the cost.

★

Book with hotel points

Marriott (Westin, W, Sheraton), Hyatt (Secrets, Dreams, Excellence, Breathless), and Hilton properties can be booked with points. A well-timed redemption cuts the resort cost by 30–50%. This is the single highest-leverage move available for all-inclusive travel.

☀

Travel in May or early June

Before hurricane season and before summer crowds. Prices drop, weather is still excellent, and resorts are noticeably less crowded. It is one of the genuinely underused travel windows for Caribbean and Mexico all-inclusives.

🌸

Adults-only for couples

Secrets, Excellence, Breathless, Zoetry. Better service, quieter pools, more elevated food. Usually $50–100/night more than family resorts. Worth it when there are no kids in the party. The difference in experience is real.

🚕

Book airport transfers independently

Skip the resort transfer desk. Cancun Transfers, Lomas Travel, or similar operators charge 20–40% less than resort-arranged private transfers. Book in advance. The resort markup on this line item is one of the more avoidable costs in the budget.

Common questions

All-inclusive cost questions, answered.

What is the average cost of an all-inclusive vacation for a party of 4?
Budget $5,500–8,000 total for a 7-night all-inclusive for a party of 4, including flights, resort, airport transfers, tips, excursions, and a 10% buffer. The resort rate alone is $2,800–4,500 per week depending on destination and property tier. Most people plan for the resort rate and then spend 15–20% more than expected on the surrounding costs.
Are all-inclusive resorts actually all-inclusive?
The resort rate covers meals, drinks, pools, beach, and basic non-motorized water sports. It typically does not cover airport transfers, off-resort excursions, spa, motorized water sports, or tips. Budget $400–700 on top of the resort rate for these even at an "all-inclusive" property. The marketing name is accurate for what it includes; it's just not exhaustive.
Which all-inclusive destination is best value?
Mexico (Cancun or Riviera Maya) offers the best combination of flight options, resort selection, and price for most US travelers. Dominican Republic (Punta Cana) is close behind with excellent beaches and competitive flight prices. Jamaica has more character but runs higher on both resort rates and airport transfer time. The "best value" question ultimately depends on where you're flying from — flight cost is the biggest variable.
