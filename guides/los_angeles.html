Money guide · Los Angeles, California

Los Angeles vacation costs: stay, eat, get around (2026)

The food is good in every neighborhood. The overrun is a rideshare to a restaurant in a different one, plus a hotel parking line you did not need.

Start here Pick Downtown, Koreatown, or Santa Monica and stay there. Porto’s or a bakery in the morning. Getty with a reservation, or one Universal day — not both, and not Disneyland.

  2. 2

    Koreatown

    B and D Lines, late food, one rideshare zone instead of a cross-town habit

  4. 4

    Near LAX

    A layover rate. The evening is a shuttle, not a neighborhood

Who this is for

For

  • People who will pick Downtown, Koreatown, or the beach and eat there
  • Anyone who wants Getty + one beach or Universal — not all three

Not for

  • Anyone stacking Disneyland from Santa Monica
  • Checklist tourists who want to “see it all” in a weekend

Getting around

Metro plus rideshare can work if you pick ONE neighborhood. A car helps if you stack a beach day and Universal — hotel parking is $40–60/night.

No rental

  • TAP card. E Line to Santa Monica; B / D Lines cover Koreatown and Downtown
  • Stay in one neighborhood. Rideshare for one planned hop, not dinner across town
  • SNA or BUR can beat LAX on a short stay — price the ground, not just the fare

With a rental

  • Hotel parking is $40–60/night — add it before you celebrate the rate
  • Still one base. Use the car for planned days (Getty, a beach, a canyon) — not 7pm dinner across town
  • Disneyland is Anaheim lodging. Do not treat it as a “quick” day from Santa Monica

Where to stay

Freehand or Ace if you are Downtown, The Line or Hotel Normandie in Koreatown, Shore Hotel if the sand is the trip. Crossing town for dinner is a second rate.

Budget

  • Freehand Downtown hostel-plus, walk to the Arts District and Metro
  • The Line or Hotel Normandie Koreatown late food, one rideshare zone

Mid-range

  • Ace Hotel Downtown Grand Central Market and the Arts District on foot
  • Shore Hotel or Palihotel Santa Monica sand plus the Expo Line

Splurge

  • Proper Santa Monica or Casa del Mar beach premium, stay west for dinner
  • 1 Hotel West Hollywood Sunset-adjacent; still one neighborhood, not a beach hop

Where to eat

Porto’s or Grand Central Market where you slept. Koreatown BBQ if that is the bed. Providence or n/naka is one tasting, not a drive from Santa Monica.

Budget

  • Porto’s or a neighborhood bakery not the hotel dining room
  • Grand Central Market or Mariscos Jalisco-class tacos if you slept Downtown

Mid-range

  • Koreatown BBQ at Quarter or Kang Ho Dong if that is the bed
  • Republique, Langer’s, or Bestia one reservation, same neighborhood

Splurge

  • Providence or n/naka one tasting, not a WeHo-to-Santa-Monica transfer
  • Skip the Casa del Mar and 1 Hotel dining rooms even on Splurge they are airport-priced

Things to do

Getty Center is free once you reserve it. Universal Studios Hollywood is the paid day, about $100–160. Griffith is the free evening. Disneyland is Anaheim lodging.

Budget

  • Getty Center reserve free entry; bus or pay parking, not a $40 drop-off
  • Griffith Observatory plus the viewpoint free, stay for sunset if you can

Mid-range

  • Universal or a studio tour one ticketed day, pick it on purpose
  • Expo Line to another neighborhood for lunch, back before rush hour

Splurge

  • Universal Express Splurge, not a default
  • Huntington or Getty Villa one garden day; Disneyland is Anaheim lodging

3-day skeleton

Day 1

The neighborhood you booked

  • Downtown: Grand Central Market and the Arts District
  • Koreatown: a bakery and barbecue on the same block as the bed
  • Santa Monica: the sand and the promenade, dinner still in Santa Monica

Day 2

Getty or Universal, not both

  • Getty Center if you reserved the free entry — bus or paid parking
  • Or Universal Studios Hollywood, about $100–160, as the only ticket
  • Griffith Observatory if you did not buy a ticket. Disneyland is not today

Day 3

The other geography, then stop

  • E Line to Santa Monica if you slept Downtown, back before rush hour
  • Or Griffith at sunset if you have not been up the hill
  • Dinner in the same zip code as the room

Book before you go

  • Getty Center reservation (free entry; pay parking or take the bus)
  • Universal or a studio tour only if that is the ticketed day
  • One dinner reservation if you want Bestia / Providence / n/naka
  • TAP card or a day fare if you will ride Metro

Hidden costs

  • Los Angeles lodging tax about 15.5%
  • Hotel parking $40–60 a night if you keep a car
  • A cross-town rideshare — $40 each way is a second dinner
  • Universal parking and Express if you add them to the gate price (about $159 at the door)
  • LAX versus BUR or SNA ground transfer

Skip this

  • Disneyland from Santa Monica — That is an Anaheim day with a long ride on both ends.
  • Casa del Mar or 1 Hotel dining rooms — Lobby prices. Porto’s and Grand Central Market exist.
  • Dinner in a different neighborhood — The ride can cost more than the plate.

One zip code, one ticketed day

Top money-saving tips

  1. Money edge: Reserve the Getty. Admission is free. A $40 rideshare each way costs more than the parking.
  2. Compare BUR or SNA ground cost with a cheap LAX fare before you book the flight.
  3. A TAP day pass before a rideshare loop. The E Line is the beach trip from Downtown.
  4. Pay $40–60 for hotel parking only on a day you are actually driving to the Getty, a canyon, or a second beach.
  5. Universal Express is the Splurge add-on. The default paid day is one general ticket, or no ticket at all.
