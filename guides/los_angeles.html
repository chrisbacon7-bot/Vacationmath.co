Who this is for

For

  • People who will pick Downtown, Koreatown, or the beach and eat there
  • Anyone who wants Getty + one beach or Universal — not all three

Not for

  • Anyone stacking Disneyland from Santa Monica
  • Checklist tourists who want to “see it all” in a weekend

Getting around

Metro plus rideshare can work if you pick ONE neighborhood. A car helps if you stack a beach day and Universal — hotel parking is $40–60/night.

No rental

  • TAP card. E Line to Santa Monica; B / D Lines cover Koreatown and Downtown
  • Stay in one neighborhood. Rideshare for one planned hop, not dinner across town
  • SNA or BUR can beat LAX on a short stay — price the ground, not just the fare

With a rental

  • Hotel parking is $40–60/night — add it before you celebrate the rate
  • Still one base. Use the car for planned days (Getty, a beach, a canyon) — not 7pm dinner across town
  • Disneyland is Anaheim lodging. Do not treat it as a “quick” day from Santa Monica

Where to stay

Pick Downtown, Koreatown, or the beach. Then stay there. Crossing town for dinner is a second hotel.

Budget

  • Freehand Downtown hostel-plus, walk to the Arts District and Metro
  • The Line or Hotel Normandie Koreatown late food, one rideshare zone

Mid-range

  • Ace Hotel Downtown Grand Central Market and the Arts District on foot
  • Shore Hotel or Palihotel Santa Monica sand plus the Expo Line

Splurge

  • Proper Santa Monica or Casa del Mar beach premium, stay west for dinner
  • 1 Hotel West Hollywood Sunset-adjacent; still one neighborhood, not a beach hop

Where to eat

Eat where you slept. Hotel restaurants in LA are airport-priced.

Budget

  • Porto’s or a neighborhood bakery not the hotel dining room
  • Grand Central Market or Mariscos Jalisco-class tacos if you slept Downtown

Mid-range

  • Koreatown BBQ at Quarter or Kang Ho Dong if that is the bed
  • Republique, Langer’s, or Bestia one reservation, same neighborhood

Splurge

  • Providence or n/naka one tasting, not a WeHo-to-Santa-Monica transfer
  • Hotel restaurants in LA are airport-priced skip them even on Splurge

Things to do

The Getty is free if you reserve. Universal Express is Splurge. Do not stack Universal, Disneyland, and a beach day.

Budget

  • Getty Center reserve free entry; bus or pay parking, not a $40 drop-off
  • Griffith Observatory plus the viewpoint free, stay for sunset if you can

Mid-range

  • Universal or a studio tour one ticketed day, pick it on purpose
  • Expo Line to another neighborhood for lunch, back before rush hour

Splurge

  • Universal Express Splurge, not a default
  • Huntington or Getty Villa one garden day; Disneyland is Anaheim lodging

3-day skeleton

Day 1

The neighborhood you booked

  • Downtown: Arts District walk, Grand Central Market, Getty if you reserved
  • Koreatown: bakery, late food, one Metro ride
  • Westside: sand, Promenade, stay west for dinner — no 6pm reservation across town

Day 2

One ticketed thing — or a second neighborhood, not both

  • Universal or a studio tour if you already priced it
  • Otherwise Griffith + a taco neighborhood, or the Expo Line for lunch and back before rush hour
  • Disneyland is not this day

Day 3

Sand or hills, then stop stacking

  • Venice / Santa Monica if you have not been west
  • Griffith and the viewpoint if you have not been up
  • Evening in the same zip code as the bed

Book before you go

  • Getty Center reservation (free entry; pay parking or take the bus)
  • Universal or a studio tour only if that is the ticketed day
  • One dinner reservation if you want Bestia / Providence / n/naka
  • TAP card or a day fare if you will ride Metro

Hidden costs

  • Hotel parking $40–60/night if you rent a car
  • Occupancy tax on the room
  • Rideshare across town — a $40 Uber each way is a second hotel night
  • Universal parking and Express if you tap yes
  • Airport transfer: LAX vs BUR vs SNA ground math
  • Bags if you split ride-shares to a compact Downtown hotel

Skip this

  • Disneyland from Santa Monica — That is an Anaheim day with a 90-minute punishment on either end.
  • Hotel restaurants — Airport prices, lobby energy. Porto’s exists.
  • Dinner across town — A $40 rideshare each way is a second hotel night you already declined.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Pick one neighborhood (Downtown, Koreatown, or the beach) and eat there. Crossing town for dinner is a second hotel.
  2. TAP day fares beat a rideshare loop. The Getty is free if you reserve — bus or pay parking, not a $40 drop-off.
  3. Porto’s or a bakery breakfast. Hotel restaurants in LA are airport-priced.
  4. Midweek flights into LAX, BUR, or SNA. Friday arrivals cost more.
  5. Skip hotel parking unless you have a canyon or beach-hop day already priced.
  6. Disneyland is Anaheim lodging and a separate ticket.
  7. Grand Central Market and a taco truck (Mariscos Jalisco class) beat a hotel dinner.
  8. Universal Express is Splurge. Getty + Griffith + one beach day is the Budget week.
