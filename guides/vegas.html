Tuesday is the product

Vegas sells a cheap room and collects the rest at the desk. Resort fees apply even on a $40 Tuesday. You pick Center-Strip (walkable Park MGM / NYNY / Horseshoe / Bellagio class) or downtown lights — not both in three nights. The monorail is a backup. A rental car is a parking fee plus a hangover you do not need.

Base yourself here

Walk the Center-Strip, or own downtown. Do not Uber from bed to the next casino.

Lean: Ellis Island / Circa-adjacent downtown, or LINQ / Flamingo class on the Center-Strip.

Stretch: Bellagio, Wynn / Encore, or Aria / Cosmopolitan class. Still a resort-fee hotel.

Solid

Center-Strip walkable. The monorail is a backup, not a plan.

  • Park MGM no-casino-smoke tower, walk to the Bellagio fountains
  • The Venetian / Palazzo huge campus; you came to walk, not to Uber

Stretch

  • Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas Center-Strip, no casino on the lobby level

  • Wynn or Encore north Strip, nicer rooms, you will still walk or tram

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Book Tuesday–Thursday. Weekend and holiday weeks double a midweek rate before you add the resort fee.
  2. Resort fees apply even on a $40 Tuesday. Add them in your head before you celebrate the “deal.”
  3. Food halls (Park MGM / Cosmo) or Chinatown / Downtown dinner. Strip steakhouses price like airports.
  4. Walk the Center-Strip. The monorail is a backup; Ubering from bed to the next casino is a habit.
  5. Fountains, Bellagio conservatory, and a Fremont walk are free. A nightclub table is Stretch, not mid.
  6. One show, not three. Cirque or a residency leftover — the street is already a show.
  7. Pay-as-you-go drinks until you run the math. Free drinks are not a meal plan.
  8. Downtown (Circa-adjacent / Fremont) is Lean lodging if you will walk those lights and rideshare to the Strip once.
