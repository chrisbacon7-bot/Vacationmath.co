Who this is for

For

  • People who will walk Center-Strip or own downtown lights — not both in three nights
  • Anyone who will add the resort fee before celebrating a $40 rate

Not for

  • Weekend and convention weeks if you came for the “deal”
  • Anyone stacking three shows and a nightclub table

Getting around

Walk Center-Strip or own downtown lights. Skip a rental unless Red Rock is a planned day — hotel parking is a fee you do not need.

No rental

  • Center-Strip is walkable if you booked Park MGM / NYNY / Horseshoe / Bellagio class
  • Deuce / monorail if you stray north. One rideshare to Fremont, not four
  • LAS is close. You do not need a car for a Center-Strip week

With a rental

  • Hotel parking is a fee plus a hangover you do not need
  • Still one base. Use the car for a planned Red Rock day, not bed-to-casino Uber replacement
  • Downtown plus a Strip night is one rideshare, not a rental justification

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Center-Strip on foot

  • Check in, walk the fountains, eat ten minutes off the carpet
  • If you booked downtown, walk Fremont and rideshare to the Strip once — not four times

Day 2

One daytime treat, or just more walking

  • High Roller off-peak or Red Rock if you have a car
  • Otherwise the conservatory, a food hall, and the sidewalk
  • The monorail is a backup

Day 3

One show, then stop stacking

  • Cirque or a mid-room if you already priced it
  • Do not add a table and a second show
  • Fly out before Saturday if you can

Book before you go

  • Tuesday–Thursday room — check the convention calendar first
  • One show if that is the trip
  • Resort fee is not optional — add it before you book
  • Airport ride: LAS is close; skip a rental unless Red Rock is a planned day

Hidden costs

  • Resort fees on a $40 Tuesday
  • Parking if you rent a car
  • Strip steakhouse and nightclub minimums
  • Show tickets and a second “just one more”
  • Drinks that are not a meal plan
  • Bags and a late checkout fee if Saturday moved

Skip this

  • Celebrating the $40 rate — Add the resort fee in your head before you text anyone.
  • A steakhouse every night — One treat. Food halls exist.
  • Three shows and a table — The street is already a show. Splurge is one reserved seat.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Book Tuesday–Thursday. Weekend and holiday weeks double a midweek rate before you add the resort fee.
  2. Resort fees apply even on a $40 Tuesday. Add them in your head before you celebrate the “deal.”
  3. Food halls (Park MGM / Cosmo) or Chinatown / Downtown dinner. Strip steakhouses price like airports.
  4. Walk the Center-Strip. The monorail is a backup; Ubering from bed to the next casino is a habit.
  5. Fountains, Bellagio conservatory, and a Fremont walk are free. A nightclub table is Splurge, not mid.
  6. One show, not three. Cirque or a residency — the street is already a show.
  7. Pay-as-you-go drinks until you run the math. Free drinks are not a meal plan.
  8. Downtown (Circa-adjacent / Fremont) is Budget lodging if you will walk those lights and rideshare to the Strip once.
