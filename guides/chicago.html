Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Ventra day pass beats a rideshare loop. The L is the plan — Mag Mile hotels are for people who will not take it.
  2. Winter rates are the value window. Summer weekends and Lolla week are not.
  3. Doughnut or diner breakfast. Downtown hotel restaurants are Chicago-priced for the same eggs.
  4. One deep-dish if you must, then stop. Italian beef or a Fulton Market casual is the rest of the week.
  5. Architecture from the sidewalk is free. A river cruise is one ticketed leftover, not a daily habit.
  6. Art Institute or a river cruise — pick one on Mid-range. Do not stack two museums and a cruise.
  7. Midweek ORD or MDW. Friday into a holiday weekend is a different room.
  8. Fulton Market lodging if you want restaurants over the Mag Mile. One neighborhood, not two towers.
