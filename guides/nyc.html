Who this is for

For

  • People who will walk a borough and ride twice a day
  • Anyone who will treat Times Square as a transfer, not a view they paid extra to sleep under

Not for

  • Anyone who wants a rental car in Manhattan
  • People stacking three observatories and a Midtown salad every lunch

Getting around

Subway first. Outer-borough lodging on a train beats a Midtown west “deal.”

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • OMNY / MetroCard. A 7-day pass wins if you ride twice a day
  • Walk a borough; rideshare only when the last train is gone
  • LGA, JFK, and EWR are different ground math. Price the AirTrain / subway, not just the fare

Where to stay

Skip Times Square unless you like paying for the neon. Pod, citizenM, or a Brooklyn train-stop room is Budget.

Budget

  • Pod 39 or citizenM Bowery small room, walk to a train
  • The Jane or a Brooklyn 2-star on the G or L cheaper than Midtown west

Mid-range

  • Ace Hotel NoMad walk to the 6 / R / W, neighborhood restaurants
  • The Hoxton Williamsburg or The Ludlow Downtown or north Brooklyn, not Times Square

Splurge

  • 1 Hotel Central Park park-adjacent, still a walk to Midtown
  • The Public or The Greenwich Hotel Downtown flagship, not a second Midtown suite

3-day skeleton

Day 1

The borough you booked

  • If you slept Downtown or in Brooklyn: waterfront, High Line or Heights promenade
  • Bodega breakfast, dinner on the same side of the river
  • Midtown only if you are changing trains

Day 2

One museum, one neighborhood dinner

  • Met or MoMA — pick one
  • Evening in the Village, Chinatown, or the borough you have not done
  • Not three interiors and a pre-theatre prix fixe

Day 3

Ferry, or Broadway if you already priced it

  • Staten Island Ferry in daylight, then a second neighborhood walk
  • Splurge spends the night on a reserved seat, not a third observatory
  • Go home on the train you already paid for

Book before you go

  • One museum timed entry (Met or MoMA — pick one)
  • Broadway rush / lottery, or one reserved seat if that is the trip
  • Ellis / Summit only if you already picked one paid view
  • OMNY or a 7-day pass if you will ride twice a day

Hidden costs

  • NYC “resort-style” hotel fees — add them in your head
  • 14.75% lodging-tax assumption on the room
  • Airport ground: AirTrain / subway vs a taxi from JFK, LGA, or EWR
  • Broadway full-price orchestra vs rush / lottery
  • Bags if you booked a Pod and packed like a suite

Skip this

  • A Times Square hotel — You paid for neon and a resort-style fee. The train still goes there.
  • Three observatories — The ferry is the skyline. One paid view is a souvenir.
  • A week of Midtown salads — The $28 lunch is how Mid-range becomes Splurge without a nicer room.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Skip a Times Square hotel unless you like paying for the neon. Pod / citizenM / a Brooklyn train-stop room is Budget.
  2. Bodega egg-and-cheese or a bakery breakfast. The hotel dining room is a Midtown salad with a room-service markup.
  3. A 7-day unlimited or OMNY cap beats a week of single swipes if you ride twice a day.
  4. Staten Island Ferry is the free skyline. Skip a paid harbor loop on Budget.
  5. One museum with pay-what-you-wish or a timed free night. Do not stack three observatories.
  6. Midweek flights. Friday into a holiday weekend is the expensive version of the same seat.
  7. Jackson Heights, Flushing, and Chinatown over Midtown for dinner. One borough per night.
  8. Broadway rush / lottery before a full-price orchestra. Splurge is a reserved seat, not three shows.
