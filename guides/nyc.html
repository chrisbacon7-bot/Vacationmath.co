New York City vacation costs: stay, eat, get around (2026)

The subway ride is cheap. The trip gets expensive when the hotel is in the wrong neighborhood and lunch keeps landing in Midtown.

Start here Sleep on a train you will ride: citizenM on the Bowery, Pod 39, or The Hoxton in Williamsburg. Bodega egg-and-cheese in the morning. Spend on the Met, MoMA, or one Broadway seat — Times Square is a corridor, not a hotel strategy.

Who this is for

For

  • People who will walk a borough and ride twice a day
  • Anyone who will treat Times Square as a transfer, not a view they paid extra to sleep under

Not for

  • Anyone who wants a rental car in Manhattan
  • People stacking three observatories and a Midtown salad every lunch

Getting around

OMNY on the subway under the hotel covers the city, and the cap is about $35 after 12 local rides on the same card. A Manhattan garage plus a tunnel is a different vacation.

You usually don’t need a car.

Transit-first

  • OMNY / MetroCard. A 7-day pass wins if you ride twice a day
  • Walk a borough; rideshare only when the last train is gone
  • LGA, JFK, and EWR are different ground math. Price the AirTrain / subway, not just the fare

Where to stay

Pod 39, citizenM Bowery, Ace Hotel NoMad, or The Hoxton Williamsburg. Times Square rents you the neon and a resort-style fee.

  • Motto by Hilton Chelsea or Courtyard Manhattan/Chelsea the 1 or the A/C/E
  • Hampton Inn Times Square Central only if the train downstairs is the point, not the neon

Mid-range

  • Ace Hotel NoMad the neighborhood over a convention tower
  • Hyatt Grand Central or The Westin Grand Central you arrived on a train
  • The Hoxton Williamsburg one river, a lower night than Midtown

Splurge

Where to eat

Bodega egg-and-cheese or a bakery. One dinner at L’Artusi, Russ & Daughters Cafe, or farther out on the 7. Le Bernardin or Via Carota is the reservation, not the daily lunch.

Budget

  • Bodega egg-and-cheese or a bakery not the hotel
  • Xi'an Famous Foods or a $1–3 slice
  • A Jackson Heights or Flushing dinner on the 7

Mid-range

  • Russ & Daughters or Katz's share it, then go to a park
  • L'Artusi if you slept downtown
  • Cafe Sabarsky once, not a pre-theatre prix fixe every night

Splurge

  • Le Bernardin the reservation
  • Via Carota or Lilia one night, booked
  • Carbone-class only if that table is why you came

Things to do

High Line and the Staten Island Ferry are the free skyline. Pay for the Met, MoMA, or a seat at the Gershwin or the Majestic — not three observatories.

Budget

  • The High Line and a Lower East Side walk
  • Staten Island Ferry free skyline, no ticket
  • A pay-what-you-wish Met hour for New York residents, or skip the museum

Mid-range

  • MoMA or the Met one
  • Brooklyn Heights and Dumbo on the train you already tapped
  • OMNY on the same card until the cap, about $35 after 12 local rides

Splurge

  • A reserved seat at the Gershwin or the Majestic
  • Summit, Edge, or Top of the Rock one deck, not three
  • A Broadway rush or lottery before a full-price orchestra seat

3-day skeleton

Day 1

Downtown on foot

  • Bodega breakfast, then the High Line or a Lower East Side walk
  • One museum if it is on this side of town
  • Dinner at L’Artusi or Russ & Daughters Cafe — not a Midtown salad

Day 2

One borough dinner

  • The 7 to Jackson Heights or Flushing for the meal
  • Or Chinatown if you are already downtown
  • Back on the same train. No third observatory

Day 3

Ferry, or one Broadway seat

  • Staten Island Ferry in daylight
  • A seat at the Gershwin or the Majestic if that ticket is the night
  • Home on the train you already tapped

Book before you go

  • One museum timed entry (Met or MoMA — pick one)
  • Broadway rush / lottery, or one reserved seat if that is the trip
  • Ellis / Summit only if you already picked one paid view
  • OMNY or a 7-day pass if you will ride twice a day

Hidden costs

  • New York lodging tax about 14.75%
  • A hotel resort-style fee on top of some advertised rates
  • Three observation decks stacked on one card
  • Broadway fees on top of the seat price
  • A rideshare when the train you slept on already goes there

Skip this

  • A Times Square hotel — You paid for a neighborhood you will leave every morning.
  • Summit, Edge, and Top of the Rock — The ferry already did the skyline.
  • A hotel dining room at lunch — That is the $28 salad, three days in a row.

The borough is the budget

Top money-saving tips

  1. Hidden gem: OMNY caps subway and local bus at about $35 over seven days, after 12 paid rides on the same card. You do not buy the old unlimited MetroCard up front.
  2. Hidden gem: The Staten Island Ferry is free, day and night, no ticket. Summit, Edge, and Top of the Rock are three paid skies.
  3. Hidden gem: One dinner on the 7 in Jackson Heights or Flushing costs less than three $28 Midtown salads. It is the same fare as a ride to Times Square.
  4. A Friday arrival in the neighborhood you already picked is a different room rate than Tuesday.
  5. Cafe Sabarsky once. A pre-theatre prix fixe every night is a second show you did not buy.
