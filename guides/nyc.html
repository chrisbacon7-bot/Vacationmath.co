The subway is the city

Your hotel is a train stop with a bed. You walk a borough, ride twice a day, and treat Times Square as a transfer, not a view you paid extra to sleep under. Outer-borough lodging on a train beats a Midtown west “deal” that still charges a resort-style fee. One borough per night for dinner. The skyline is free from the Staten Island Ferry.

Base yourself here

Sleep on a train, not under a billboard.

Budget: Pod / citizenM / Freehand, or an outer-borough 2-star on a line. Brooklyn or Queens beats Midtown west.

Splurge: 1 Hotel Central Park if you want the park; The Public or Greenwich Hotel class if you want Downtown. One flagship, not a second suite.

Three days if you refuse the checklist

Walk a borough. Ride twice. Eat where you already are.

Day 1

The borough you booked

If you slept Downtown or in Brooklyn: waterfront, High Line or Heights promenade, bodega breakfast, dinner on the same side of the river. Midtown only if you are changing trains.

Day 2

One museum, one neighborhood dinner

Met or MoMA — pick one. Evening in the Village, Chinatown, or the borough you have not done. Not three interiors and a pre-theatre prix fixe you will not remember.

Day 3

Ferry, or Broadway if you have room in the budget

Staten Island Ferry in daylight, then a second neighborhood walk. Stretch spends the night on a reserved seat, not a third observatory. Go home on the train you already paid for.

Keep the number honest

Top money-saving tips

  1. Skip a Times Square hotel unless you like paying for the neon. Pod / citizenM / a Brooklyn train-stop room is Lean.
  2. Bodega egg-and-cheese or a bakery breakfast. The hotel dining room is a Midtown salad with room-service prices.
  3. A 7-day unlimited or OMNY cap beats a week of single swipes if you ride twice a day.
  4. Staten Island Ferry is the free skyline. Skip a paid harbor loop on Lean.
  5. One museum with pay-what-you-wish or a timed free night. Do not stack three observatories.
  6. Midweek flights. Friday into a holiday weekend is the expensive version of the same seat.
  7. Jackson Heights, Flushing, and Chinatown over Midtown for dinner. One borough per night.
  8. Broadway rush / lottery before a full-price orchestra. Stretch is a reserved seat, not three shows.
