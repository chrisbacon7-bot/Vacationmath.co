Trip Plan FAQ

How is this different from Trip Finder and Budget Reverse Math?

Trip Plan starts with a destination you already want and a hard budget, then builds one constrained plan. Trip Finder starts with a date window and ranks destinations. Budget Reverse Math starts with a budget and ranks trip types. Use this page when the place is decided and the number is the constraint.

How many destinations can I plan?

70+ from the same 2026 Trip Finder catalog — Caribbean, Europe, Hawaii, domestic US, Asia, and more — plus Walt Disney World and a Caribbean cruise. Type in the filter, jump from the shortcuts, or scroll the region groups.

What does Fits, Tight, and Over mean?

Fits means the recommended plan lands comfortably under your budget (about 8% of room or more). Tight means you are within about 8% either side — doable if you watch the optional lines. Over means the recommended plan needs cuts, or a bigger number, before it is honest.

Does the Disney plan include Florida taxes?

Yes. Orange County lodging tax of 12.5% is applied to Disney resort rooms. Florida sales tax of 6.5% is applied to park tickets and Lightning Lane. Those two lines are the ones most room-plus-tickets quotes skip.

Are cruise gratuities included?

Yes, as their own line — not buried in the cabin fare. Automatic daily gratuities apply to guests age 2 and up, scaled to your nights, using the same 2026 per-line rates as the cruise calculator.

What is included in an all-inclusive plan?

Cancún, Punta Cana, Jamaica, Aruba, Turks & Caicos, the Bahamas, St. Lucia, Cabo, Tulum, Costa Rica, and Belize use the dedicated all-inclusive rates: package + flights + customary tips + two excursions. Other cities use Trip Finder hotel + daily ground rates, occupancy tax, airfare, and a 6% planning buffer. See the all-inclusive calculator for the deeper split.

What is Where to book?

It sits next to Lean / Solid / Stretch, before the itemized plan. Tabs cover Hotel, Food, Activities, Getting there (airline + airports), and Season. Hotel is first because that is usually the points booking. Season uses the Trip Finder monthly hotel band (or Disney / cruise season tables) to name the cheapest open months and the months to skip. Hotel lists example property or cabin names for the style you picked — not a ranking and not live inventory. Getting there is a pattern from your origin hub and region — never a flight number. Food and activities are curated names for major destinations. No affiliate links.

What are the suggestions under each line item?

Every major line has one short suggestion for the selected Lean / Solid / Stretch tier. The gray note is the assumption. Longer hotel / food / activity lists live in Where to book so the table stays scannable. No live inventory and no flight numbers.

Are these live quotes?

No. These are honest 2026 estimates built from the same published pricing tables as the other Vacation Math calculators. Real bookings can move. Use the destination calculators before you pay a deposit, and plan the funding if the number is tight.
