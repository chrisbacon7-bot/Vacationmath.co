The Gobbler

Someone at the table is eating the feast. A phone game for Thanksgiving. Secret roles. Secret missions. No app, no account, no TV.

How to play

  1. 1One phone starts a table and shares a short code. Everyone else joins with a name.
  2. 2You are a Pilgrim, or you are the Gobbler. Hold your phone close to peek at the role.
  3. 3Each course, do a secret mission. When a Gobbler marks theirs done, a dish disappears.
  4. 4Hit Gobble! to accuse someone. At pie time, the table names the Gobbler.

The live table needs JavaScript. It is already on. If the buttons never show, turn JavaScript on and refresh.
