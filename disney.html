Your Disney trip

Disney uses 10+ for adult tickets. Under 3 is free on tickets, dining, and Lightning Lane.

Multi-day tickets get cheaper per day (a 7-day is not 7× a 1-day). Florida 6.5% tax is added. Lodging tax is 12.5% (All-Star 13.5%).

Use this for DVC/points rental or a quoted hotel. DVC usually lands near Moderate cash for a Deluxe villa — enter the cash-equivalent nightly rate, not points. Lodging tax is not added on custom/DVC (your number may already include it).

How we got these numbers

Every line item is built from public 2026 pricing data (updated September 2026) — estimates, not live quotes. The biggest variances come from season (peak adds 30-50%), resort tier (deluxe is ~3-4x value), and dining style.
